# The Three Seats of Brightshore

|Guild|Domain|Alignment Tendencies|Reputation|
|---|---|---|---|
|The Golden Sands|Wealth, Trade, Mining|Lawful Evil|Ambitious|
|The Iron Ledger|Industry, Resources, Infrastructure|Lawful Neutral|Dependable|
|The Silver Fins|Shipping, Exploration, Maritime Trade|Neutral|Adventurous|

No guild possesses enough influence to rule alone, forcing them into a constant game of alliances, favours, and sabotage.

# The Golden Sands

### Public Face

The Golden Sands present themselves as visionaries.

Their leaders speak of progress, prosperity, and expansion. They sponsor festivals, fund public works, and donate generously to temples.

Most ordinary citizens appreciate them because business is usually good when the Golden Sands are involved.

### Private Reality

Their motto might as well be:

> "Everything has a price."

The discovery of Shadowshard Crystals has made them incredibly aggressive. They seek exclusive mining rights around Hollowcove and quietly pressure local officials to grant them monopolies.

Guild agents routinely:

- Buy political influence.
    
- Manipulate markets.
    
- Acquire competitors through debt.
    
- Employ adventurers for morally questionable tasks.
    

### Relationship with Others

**Iron Ledger**

- Necessary partner.
    
- Controls mining equipment and construction.
    
- Frequent commercial rivals.
    

**Silver Fins**

- Depend on their ships.
    
- Constantly attempt to undercut them.
    

---

# The Iron Ledger

### Public Face

The Iron Ledger prides itself on stability.

They built many of Brightshore's roads, warehouses, quarries, docks, and defensive walls.

Citizens trust them because they rarely make promises they cannot keep.

### Internal Culture

The Ledger operates almost like a military organization.

Ranks are clearly defined.

Records are obsessively maintained.

Contracts are sacred.

Breaking a signed agreement with the Ledger can ruin someone's reputation for decades.

### Current Concern

The [[Shadowshard Crystal]] rush worries them.

Golden Sands wants rapid expansion.

The Ledger fears reckless mining could destabilize the region and destroy existing industries.

Many senior members advocate slower development.

### Relationship with Others

**Golden Sands**

- Reckless profiteers.
    

**Silver Fins**

- Useful transport partners.
    
- Often easier to negotiate with.
    

---

# The Silver Fins

### Public Face

The Silver Fins are sailors first and merchants second.

Their guild halls are filled with maps, trophies, navigation charts, and stories gathered from distant lands.

They're often seen as more approachable than the other two guilds.

### Internal Culture

The guild values initiative.

Captains enjoy significant independence.

Success is measured less by rank and more by achievement.

A newly discovered trade route can elevate a captain's status overnight.

### Current Concern

The Silver Fins are fascinated by the caves beneath the bay.

Several exploratory vessels have vanished investigating the deeper cave systems near the Singing Stones.

Some captains believe new trade routes or valuable resources lie below.

Others fear something ancient stirs in the depths.

### Relationship with Others

**Golden Sands**

- Useful customers.
    
- Dangerous allies.
    

**Iron Ledger**

- Reliable but unimaginative.

# Political Tension in the Campaign

At the start of the campaign, the factions might appear cooperative.

As players investigate Hollowcove and the [[Shadowshard Crystal]] mystery, they slowly discover:

- Golden Sands is exploiting the discovery.
    
- Iron Ledger suspects something is wrong underground.
    
- Silver Fins has lost ships investigating nearby caves.
    
- All three factions possess pieces of the truth.
    
- None of them understand the full danger.
