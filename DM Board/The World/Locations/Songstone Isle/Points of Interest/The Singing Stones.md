## Overview
The Singing Stones are an ancient natural wonder, the origin of which is lost to time. They are enormous chime-like stones that in the center of  [[Brightshore Bay]]. They float roughly 30 meters above the water's surface, and emit a low hum. Directly below the stones the water glows with a brilliant blue-green light that makes the stones stand out like a natural lighthouse at night.
