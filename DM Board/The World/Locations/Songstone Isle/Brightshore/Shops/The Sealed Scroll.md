# The Sealed Scroll

## Overview

The Sealed Scroll is one of Brightshore's most respected repositories of knowledge. Situated near the harbor district, the shop serves as a bookstore, mapmaker, navigator's guildhall, and information exchange all at once.

Owned by the Silver Fins, the establishment specializes in maps, nautical charts, travel journals, exploration records, and rare texts collected from across Yá'rael.

Unlike most bookshops, The Sealed Scroll's greatest treasures are often hidden behind the counter rather than displayed on shelves. Captains, explorers, adventurers, and scholars frequently visit seeking information unavailable anywhere else.

The shop's motto is engraved above the entrance:

> _"Every Voyage Begins With a Single Line."_

---

# Appearance

## Exterior Description

The building is a narrow three-story structure of white stone and dark timber overlooking one of Brightshore's busiest docks.

Large circular windows resemble ship portholes.

A carved wooden sign depicts a rolled scroll sealed with blue wax and tied with silver cord.

Weather vanes shaped like sailing ships rotate atop the roof.

---

## Interior Layout

The interior resembles a cross between a library and a ship captain's study.

Tall shelves reach nearly to the ceiling.

Maps hang from walls, rafters, and display frames.

Several large tables dominate the center of the room, covered in charts, compasses, rulers, and navigation tools.

# Owner & Staff

## Corwin Tidequill

A middle-aged half-elf scholar and former navigator.

Corwin spent decades sailing with Silver Fins expeditions before retiring ashore.

He speaks calmly, remembers names with alarming accuracy, and seems to know something about everyone.

### Personality

- Patient
- Intelligent
- Curious
- Observant
- Secretive

## Elsie Wren

Human archivist.

Responsible for cataloguing the shop's enormous collection.

A gnome cartographer.

Produces detailed maps and copies charts for customers.

Originally founded by retired explorers and navigators.

## Major Events

- Survived multiple harbor fires.
- Expanded following the discovery of Shadowshard Crystals.
- Became the premier navigation center in the region.

---

## Reputation Over Time

The Sealed Scroll has developed a reputation as one of Brightshore's most trustworthy sources of information.

Many captains refuse to leave port without consulting its charts.
