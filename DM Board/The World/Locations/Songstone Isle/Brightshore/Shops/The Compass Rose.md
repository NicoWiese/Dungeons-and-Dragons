# `=this.file.name`

## Overview

The Compass Rose is Brightshore's premier supplier of maps, navigation tools, and travel equipment. Sailors, explorers, merchants, and adventurers regularly visit before departing on voyages throughout the [[Singing Stone Isles]].

## Appearance

- Two-story stone-and-timber building near the harbor.
    
- A large brass compass hangs above the entrance.
    
- Walls covered with maps and nautical charts.
    
- Shelves lined with journals, sextants, compasses, and spyglasses.
    
- Smells of parchment, ink, and seawater.
    
- Constant scratching of quills and quiet discussion among travelers.
    

## Owner & Staff

- **Corwin Saltmark**
    
    - Former ship navigator.
        
    - Friendly, knowledgeable, and endlessly curious.
        
    - Loves hearing stories from distant lands.
        
    - Secretly maintains copies of every map he sells.
        
- **Mira Saltmark**
    
    - Daughter and apprentice cartographer.
        
    - Exceptionally talented artist.
        
    - Often accompanies exploration expeditions.
        
- **Jeb Finn**
    
    - Clerk and porter.
        
    - Knows nearly every captain in Brightshore.
        

## Goods & Inventory

- Regional maps.
    
- Sea charts.
    
- Compasses.
    
- Sextants.
    
- Journals and logbooks.
    
- Travel packs.
    
- Waterproof map cases.
    
- Local guidebooks.
    

## Specialties

- Custom-made maps.
    
- Updated charts of the [[Singing Stone Isles]].
    
- Navigation advice.
    
- Copies of explorer journals.
    
- Rare historical maps.
    

## Services

- Buying and selling navigation equipment.
    
- Map creation and copying.
    
- Route planning.
    
- Appraisal of maps and journals.
    
- Information gathering from sailors and explorers.
    

## Prices & Trade

- Fair prices compared to most Brightshore merchants.
    
- Small discounts for members of [[The Silver Fins]].
    
- Haggling is accepted for large purchases.
    
- Trade accepted for maps, journals, and exploration notes.
    
- Rare maps command premium prices.
    

## Notable NPCs

- Captains preparing for voyages.
    
- Adventurers seeking unexplored locations.
    
- Representatives of [[The Silver Fins]].
    
- Scholars researching the islands.
    

## Rumors & Hooks

- A recently recovered ship's log mentions an uncharted island.
    
- Several maps disagree about the location of a reef near [[The Kraken's Maw]].
    
- Corwin is looking for someone to verify a newly discovered route.
    
- An explorer vanished after purchasing an old chart.
    

## Events

- Monthly explorer gatherings.
    
- Auctions of recovered maps.
    
- Navigation competitions during festivals.
    
- Occasional disputes over map ownership.
    

## Rooms & Layout

- Main showroom.
    
- Map gallery.
    
- Storage room.
    
- Cartography workshop.
    
- Private office and archive.
    

## Secrets

- Corwin possesses several maps considered too dangerous to sell publicly.
    
- Hidden within the archive is a collection of charts recovered from shipwrecks.
    
- Some maps show islands that no longer appear on modern charts.
    

## History

- Founded by Corwin after retiring from life at sea.
    
- Became popular during the expansion of trade throughout the islands.
    
- Frequently used by explorers heading toward newly discovered locations.
    
- Known as one of the most reliable sources of navigation information in Brightshore.
    

## Additional Notes

- Excellent location for introducing exploration quests.
    
- Corwin can provide rumors about nearly any coastal settlement.
    
- A useful source of maps, leads, and adventure hooks.
