# `=this.file.name`

## Overview

The Gilded Exchange is one of Brightshore's most prestigious mercantile establishments and serves as an unofficial commercial hub for [[The Golden Sands]]. Wealthy merchants, ship captains, nobles, and visiting dignitaries regularly conduct business within its polished halls.

While common goods can be found here, the shop's true purpose is connecting buyers and sellers of rare, exotic, and expensive merchandise from across Yá'rael.

## Appearance

### Exterior Description

A three-story sandstone building overlooking the harbor. Large glass windows display imported goods and colorful banners bearing the symbol of [[The Golden Sands]].

### Interior Layout

The main floor is dedicated to luxury goods and merchant services, while upper floors contain private meeting rooms and offices.

### Displays, Shelves, Counters

Polished hardwood shelves display fine wares from distant lands. Locked glass cabinets protect valuable jewelry and curiosities.

### Lighting, Smells, Sounds

Warm lantern light illuminates the shop. The air smells faintly of spices, polished wood, and imported perfumes. Quiet conversations and the scratching of quills fill the background.

## History

Founded shortly after [[The Golden Sands]] secured its first council seats in Brightshore, The Gilded Exchange was intended to showcase the guild's growing influence and prosperity.

Over the years it has become one of the city's most recognizable commercial institutions and a symbol of Golden Sands ambition.

## Additional Notes

- Dockworkers often refer to the building as "The Golden Palace."
    
- Most major trade deals in Brightshore pass through its halls at some point.
    
- Adventurers seeking rare equipment often begin their search here.
    
- Even those who cannot afford its goods visit simply to admire the merchandise.
