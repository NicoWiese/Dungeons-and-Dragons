# Sticks n' Stones

## Overview

Nestled between the warehouses and lower merchant district of Brightshore stands Sticks n' Stones, a humble smithy fighting to survive in a city increasingly controlled by powerful merchant guilds.

Its owner, the dwarven smith Borin Ashforge, was once known for producing some of the finest tools and weapons on the Singing Stone Isles. However, after years of being squeezed out of lucrative material contracts by larger guild-backed competitors, Borin has been forced to adapt.

Unable to consistently acquire fresh ore and quality steel, he now purchases damaged armor, broken weapons, shipwreck salvage, and discarded tools, melting them down and reforging them into reliable equipment.

Though lacking the prestige of Brightshore's wealthier forges, Sticks n' Stones has earned a loyal customer base among sailors, miners, adventurers, and working folk who value durability over appearances.

---

## Appearance

### Exterior Description

The shop occupies a sturdy white-stone building stained with soot and decades of hard work. A weathered wooden sign hangs above the entrance depicting a crossed stick and hammer over an anvil.

Unlike the polished storefronts found elsewhere in Brightshore, Sticks n' Stones appears practical and worn. Metal scraps, wagon wheels, anchors, and bundles of salvaged equipment are neatly stacked outside beneath a covered awning.

The constant ringing of hammer strikes can often be heard half a street away.

### Interior Layout

The front room serves as a combination showroom and trading floor.

Weapon racks line the walls while armor stands display repaired and restored pieces. Shelves hold tools, fishing gear, hardware, and assorted metal goods.

A broad counter separates customers from the workshop beyond.

### Displays, Shelves, and Counters

- Reforged swords displayed on wooden racks.
- Barrels filled with scrap metal awaiting sorting.
- Tool displays organized by profession.
- Armor mannequins showcasing repaired suits.
- A glass cabinet containing Borin's finest work.

### Lighting, Smells, and Sounds

- Warm orange light from the forge.
- Smell of coal smoke and hot iron.
- Constant clinking of metal.
- Hissing steam from cooling barrels.
- Occasional dwarven work songs sung by Borin.

## Owner & Staff

### Borin Ashforge

A broad-shouldered dwarf with a soot-black beard threaded with silver rings.

Borin is stubborn, hardworking, and fiercely proud of his craft. Though frustrated by his declining fortunes, he refuses to lower his standards.

He believes every piece of metal deserves a second life.

### Hilda Ashforge

Borin's wife and business manager.

Practical, sharp-tongued, and excellent with numbers, Hilda handles contracts and keeps the shop solvent.

She openly distrusts merchant guilds.

### Nib Underbough

A young halfling apprentice.

Energetic and curious, Nib spends his days sorting salvage and learning smithing techniques.

He frequently gets into trouble exploring parts of the city he shouldn't.

### Hidden Agendas, Debts, and Secrets

- Borin owes a significant debt to a private lender.
- Hilda is secretly searching for alternative ore suppliers.
- Nib occasionally sneaks into abandoned caves near the harbor.

## Specialties

### Signature Products

#### Tideforged Steel

Borin's trademark craftsmanship.

Created by combining salvaged maritime steel from dozens of sources, Tideforged equipment is exceptionally durable and resistant to corrosion.

### Custom Work

- Personalized weapons.
- Family heirloom restoration.
- Custom armor fitting.
- Engraving services.

### Magical, Exotic, or Illegal Items

Borin refuses to knowingly produce illegal goods.

However, he occasionally repairs unusual items brought by adventurers without asking too many questions.

### Famous Items

The shop is best known for:

- Tideforged Cutlasses.
- Deepwater Harpoons.

## Secrets

### Hidden Debt

Borin's financial situation is worse than most realize.

A creditor plans to seize the property if payments are missed.

### Sabotaged Contracts

Evidence suggests someone within Brightshore is deliberately interfering with Borin's supply network.

### The Locked Chest

A reinforced iron chest hidden in the cellar contains fragments of an unusual black-veined metal recovered from a shipwreck decades ago.

Borin has never been able to identify it.

### Secret Connection

Several fragments appear eerily similar to refined [[Shadowshard Crystal]] alloys.

---

## History

### Founding Story

Founded nearly fifty years ago by Borin and Hilda shortly after arriving in Brightshore.

### Past Success

The forge once supplied tools to much of the surrounding region.

### Decline

The rise of guild-controlled supply chains gradually weakened independent craftsmen.

### Current Reputation

Though struggling financially, Sticks n' Stones remains one of the most respected independent forges in Brightshore.

Many locals hope Borin survives the growing economic conflict engulfing the city.
