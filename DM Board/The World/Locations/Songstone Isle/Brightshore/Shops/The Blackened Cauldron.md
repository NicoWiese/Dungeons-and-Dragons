# `=this.file.name`

## Overview

The Blackened Cauldron sits tucked away in one of Brightshore's older streets, wedged between a cobbler and a weatherworn boarding house. Its crooked chimney constantly emits colorful smoke, making it easy to find and impossible to ignore.

The shop is owned by Agatha Bramblebrew, a kind-hearted but undeniably eccentric old woman who has become something of a local legend. She provides medicines, potions, remedies, and advice to anyone who asks, whether they can afford it or not.

Unfortunately, recent years have been difficult.

As merchant guilds tighten their control over trade routes and suppliers, Agatha has found it increasingly difficult to acquire the rare herbs and ingredients she needs. What once arrived regularly now arrives late, overpriced, or not at all.

Despite these hardships, she refuses to sell out to the merchant guilds and remains fiercely independent.

Most locals trust Agatha far more than any guild-backed apothecary.

# Appearance

## Exterior Description

The shop occupies a narrow stone building with a steep roof and a crooked chimney.

Bundles of drying herbs hang from every available surface.

Colorful bottles fill the windows.

A large iron cauldron hangs above the entrance, blackened by decades of use.

A painted sign reads:

> _"Potions, Poultices, Remedies, Regrets."_

Nobody knows what the last word means.

---

## Interior Layout

The shop feels equal parts apothecary, greenhouse, and wizard's workshop.

Shelves stretch from floor to ceiling.

Every available surface is occupied by jars, ingredients, books, and bubbling concoctions.

The air constantly shifts between pleasant floral aromas and alarming chemical smells.

# Owner & Staff

## Agatha Bramblebrew

An elderly human woman whose age is impossible to determine.

She possesses sharp green eyes, wild silver hair, and a habit of carrying multiple spoons for reasons she refuses to explain.

### Personality

- Kind
- Scatterbrained
- Incredibly knowledgeable
- Stubborn
- Generous

She often appears absent-minded but misses very little.

---

## Pip Mosswhistle

A young gnome apprentice.

Enthusiastic and eager to learn.

Responsible for deliveries and ingredient preparation.

Often exhausted.

---

## Mottle

A fat black cat with one torn ear.

Officially a pet.

Unofficially appears to supervise the entire operation.

Most customers are convinced Mottle is smarter than many people.

---
