# Halloway's Fishery

## Overview

Run by father and son, Shoggokh and Mukgash Thakbruz, Halloway's Fishery is one of Brightshore's most respected seafood merchants. The business has served the city for generations and is particularly popular among wealthy households, taverns, and visiting merchants seeking the finest catches from the Singing Stone Isles.

## Appearance

- A large stone-and-timber storefront overlooking the harbor.
    
- Fresh catches displayed on beds of ice beneath canvas awnings.
    
- Wooden counters and polished display cases.
    
- The scent of saltwater, smoked fish, and sea herbs fills the air.
    
- Constant sounds of bargaining, chopping, and dockworkers delivering new catches.
    

## Owner & Staff

### Shoggokh Thakbruz

- Elderly but sturdy half-orc fisherman.
    
- Proud of the family's reputation.
    
- Values honesty and hard work above profit.
    
- Knows most local captains by name.
    

### Mukgash Thakbruz

- Son and eventual heir to the business.
    
- Ambitious and business-minded.
    
- Interested in expanding operations beyond Brightshore.
    
- Often handles negotiations with merchants.
    

### Rilka Fellows

- Long-serving clerk and bookkeeper.
    
- Keeps meticulous financial records.
    
- Secretly knows every piece of harbor gossip.
    

## Goods & Inventory

### Common Goods

- Fresh local fish.
    
- Smoked seafood.
    
- Salted preservation rations.
    
- Crab, lobster, and shellfish.
    

### Unusual Items

- Rainbow Tide specialty catches.
    
- Rare deepwater fish.
    
- Decorative fish scales and bones.
    

### Seasonal Goods

- Songstone Whale byproducts (when legally obtained).
    
- Festival-exclusive seafood platters.
    
- Large Rainbow Tide catches.
    

## Specialties

- Premium cuts of [[Starlight Salmon]].
    
- Carefully prepared [[Petalfish]] fillets.
    
- Assorted seafood platters supplied to Brightshore's finest taverns.
    
- Custom orders for noble households and merchant guilds.
    

## Services

- Fresh seafood sales.
    
- Bulk orders for taverns and ships.
    
- Fish cleaning and preparation.
    
- Long-term preservation through smoking and salting.
    
- Supply contracts for merchants and captains.
    

## Prices & Trade

- Slightly above average prices.
    
- Quality is considered worth the cost.
    
- Minor haggling accepted for large purchases.
    
- Bulk discounts available.
    
- Barter accepted from trusted fishermen.
    

## Notable NPCs

- Harbor captains regularly visit for supplies.
    
- Chefs from prominent Brightshore taverns purchase stock daily.
    
- Representatives from merchant guilds occasionally negotiate contracts.
    

## Rumors & Hooks

- Several fishermen claim unusual fish have been appearing near the Singing Stones.
    
- A recent shipment of expensive seafood never arrived at the docks.
    
- Some sailors insist fish caught near certain reefs have black blood.
    

## Events

- Major sales during the Rainbow Tide Festival.
    
- Seasonal seafood competitions.
    
- Annual fishing contests sponsored by local merchants.
    

## Rooms & Layout

### Shop Floor

- Main sales area and displays.
    

### Preparation Room

- Cleaning and processing station.
    

### Smokehouse

- Used for preserving fish.
    

### Cold Storage

- Ice-packed storage for valuable catches.
    

### Office

- Records, contracts, and finances.
    

## Secrets

- Shoggokh quietly provides food at reduced prices to struggling fishing families.
    
- Mukgash has been considering a trade partnership with The Golden Sands.
    
- The family occasionally purchases unusual catches from fishermen without asking too many questions.
    

## History

- Established several generations ago by the Halloway family. The shop was horrible, and when Halloway sold a member of the Thakbruz family rotten fish, they killed him and took over the shop. 
    
- Began as a small dockside stall before expanding into a respected fishery.
    
- Survived storms, economic downturns, and merchant rivalries.
    
- Maintains a strong reputation for quality and reliability.
    

## Additional Notes

- During the Rainbow Tide, queues can stretch out the door.
    
- Shoggokh is always willing to share a fishing story.
    
- Many locals consider Halloway's Fishery a Brightshore institution.
