# `=this.file.name`

## Overview

Unlike the elegant displays and adventurous atmosphere of The Sealed Scroll, Three Seas Cartography exists for one purpose:

**Accuracy.**

Operated by the Iron Ledger, the business serves as Brightshore's foremost authority on land surveys, construction planning, geological assessments, and practical cartography.

While sailors and explorers frequent The Sealed Scroll, engineers, miners, merchants, architects, and civic officials come here.

Maps produced by Three Seas Cartography are rarely beautiful.

They are, however, almost always correct.

Every road, quarry, mine, cliff face, and drainage ditch is painstakingly documented according to strict Ledger standards.

Locals often joke:

> "The Silver Fins draw maps to discover the world. The Iron Ledger draws maps so the world can be taxed."

# Appearance

## Exterior Description

The building resembles a government office more than a shop.

Constructed from clean white stone with dark slate roofing, it bears the understated sigil of the Iron Ledger above the entrance.

No colorful banners hang outside.

No displays advertise its services.

Only a brass plaque reads:

> _"Three Seas Cartography — Precision Through Knowledge."_

A pair of surveyor's tripods stand beside the doorway.

---

## Interior Layout

The interior is organized with military precision.

Everything has a designated location.

Maps are stored in labeled drawers.

Documents are archived alphabetically.

Visitors often feel compelled to stand up straighter while inside.

# Secrets

## Suppressed Maps

Several maps depicting extensive cave networks beneath the Singing Stones have been sealed by Iron Ledger leadership.

## The Lost Survey

Twenty-three years ago, an Iron Ledger survey team vanished while investigating a newly discovered cavern.

Official records state the expedition was cancelled.

The surviving notes suggest otherwise.

---

## Geological Anomalies

Recent mining operations have revealed impossible rock formations beneath Hollowcove.

Brumm suspects they predate recorded history.
