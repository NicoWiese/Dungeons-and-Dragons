# `=this.file.name`

## Overview

Prissila's Precious Pieces is Brightshore's most prestigious jeweler, catering primarily to wealthy merchants, guild officials, nobles, and visiting dignitaries. The shop's owner, Prissila Vaelora, proudly claims that every item within her establishment surpasses anything offered elsewhere in Yá'rael.

Whether this is true or not depends entirely on who is asked.

## Appearance

### Exterior Description

A pristine white-stone building stands on a prominent corner of the Merchant District. Elegant stained-glass windows shimmer with jewel-toned colors, while gold-leaf lettering displays the shop's name above polished oak doors.

A pair of decorative silver griffins flank the entrance.

### Interior Layout

The showroom is spacious and immaculately organized, divided into themed display sections featuring rings, necklaces, brooches, gemstones, and commissioned works.

Private consultation rooms line the rear wall for wealthy clients wishing to conduct business discreetly.

### Displays, Shelves, Counters

Glass display cases reinforced with subtle magical wards showcase the most valuable items. Velvet-lined shelves display finished jewelry, while raw gemstones are kept behind the main counter.

### Lighting, Smells, Sounds

Soft magical lanterns cast warm golden light across the shop.

The air smells faintly of lavender polish and expensive perfumes.

Quiet instrumental music plays continuously from an enchanted music box hidden somewhere within the building.

## Owner & Staff

### Owner Background/Personality

**Prissila Vaelora** is a high elf jeweler whose skill is rivaled only by her ego.

Elegant, refined, and endlessly critical, she firmly believes she possesses the finest artistic eye in the entire region. She is polite to wealthy clients and barely tolerates everyone else.

Despite her arrogance, her craftsmanship is genuinely exceptional.

### Staff

**Elian Duskpetal**

- Senior gem cutter.
    
- Quiet and meticulous.
    
- Handles the majority of custom gemstone work.
    

**Marra Quickquill**

- Human clerk.
    
- Manages appointments and finances.
    
- Knows nearly every wealthy customer in Brightshore.
    

**Golden Sands Guards**

- Officially security personnel.
    
- Unofficially ensure guild interests are protected.
    

### Hidden Agendas, Debts, or Secrets

Prissila owes much of her success to exclusive trade contracts negotiated through The Golden Sands.

If those agreements vanished, her business would suffer significantly.

## Specialties

### Signature Products

Prissila's signature line consists of silver-and-shadow crystal jewelry that catches and refracts light in unusual ways.

### Custom Work

Nobles regularly commission:

- Family crests
    
- Engagement jewelry
    
- Ceremonial regalia
    
- Guild insignias
    

### Magical, Exotic, or Illegal Items

Occasionally, wealthy patrons commission enchanted accessories through third-party enchanters.

Prissila publicly refuses illegal commissions.

### Items the Shop is Famous For

The "Sea's Radiance" collection, crafted using pearls and crystals harvested near the Shimmering Reef.

## Services

### Buying and Selling

The shop purchases:

- Gems
    
- Jewelry
    
- Precious metals
    
- Noble heirlooms
    

### Repairs

Repairs range from simple resizing to complete restoration of damaged jewelry.

### Commissions

Most custom orders require a substantial deposit.

### Appraisals

Prissila offers highly respected appraisals used throughout Brightshore's merchant community.

### Crafting or Enchanting

The shop works closely with local enchanters for magical commissions.

### Information Brokering

Wealthy clients often exchange rumors while conducting business, making the shop an excellent place to gather information.

## Prices & Trade

### Typical Pricing

- Common Jewelry: 25–200 gp
    
- Fine Jewelry: 200–1,500 gp
    
- Masterwork Pieces: 1,500+ gp
    

### Haggling Policy

Prissila considers haggling "uncultured behavior."

Attempts generally increase prices.

### Barter Options

Only accepted for rare gemstones, historical artifacts, or luxury goods.

### Discounts or Markups

Members of The Golden Sands receive modest discounts.

### Credit, Favors, or Special Arrangements

Trusted noble clients may purchase on credit.

- Prissila possesses documentation revealing which nobles secretly owe money to merchant guilds.
    

## History

Prissila arrived in Brightshore nearly fifty years ago with little more than her skills and a small collection of gemstones.

Through talent, determination, and strategic partnerships with The Golden Sands, she steadily built her reputation.

The discovery of Shadowshard Crystals elevated the shop into one of the city's most profitable luxury businesses.

Today, Prissila's Precious Pieces is considered the premier jeweler in Brightshore.

## Additional Notes

- The shop serves complimentary tea to wealthy clients.
