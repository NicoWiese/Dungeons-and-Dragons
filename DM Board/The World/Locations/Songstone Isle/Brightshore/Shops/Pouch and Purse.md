# Pouch and Purse

## Overview

Run by Ricard Grist, Pouch and Purse specializes in luxury goods, rare curiosities, and expensive trinkets imported from across Yá'rael. Wealthy merchants, visiting nobles, and collectors regularly browse its shelves in search of unique treasures.

## Appearance

- Polished stone exterior with large glass display windows.
    
- Rich wooden shelving and velvet-lined display cases.
    
- Brass lanterns provide warm lighting.
    
- Smells faintly of cedarwood and old parchment.
    
- Quiet atmosphere with soft music playing throughout the shop.
    

## Owner & Staff

- **Ricard Grist**
    
    - Successful merchant and skilled appraiser.
        
    - Polite, charming, and always calculating value.
        
    - Strong ties to [[The Golden Sands]].
        
- **Elira Voss**
    
    - Senior clerk.
        
    - Expert in imported goods.
        
- **Bram Nettles**
    
    - Apprentice and stockkeeper.
        
    - Frequently misplaces inventory.
        

## Goods & Inventory

- Jewelry and gemstones.
    
- Decorative statuettes.
    
- Imported artwork.
    
- Antique navigational instruments.
    
- Collectible coins and relics.
    
- Rare books and maps.
    

## Specialties

- Luxury gifts for nobles and merchants.
    
- Imported curiosities from distant ports.
    
- Expert appraisal of valuables.
    
- Hard-to-find collectibles.
    

## Services

- Buying and selling valuables.
    
- Appraisal services.
    
- Special orders and commissions.
    
- Pawn services for trusted customers.
    

## Prices & Trade

- Prices are generally higher than average.
    
- Haggling is accepted but difficult.
    
- Rare items often carry significant markups.
    
- Credit available for established clients.
    

## Notable NPCs

- Wealthy collectors.
    
- Merchant captains.
    
- Representatives of [[The Golden Sands]].
    
- Visiting nobles.
    

## Rumors & Hooks

- A collector is searching for a missing artifact.
    
- Ricard recently purchased something recovered from a shipwreck.
    
- Several unusual buyers have been asking about ancient relics.
    

## Events

- Seasonal auctions.
    
- Noble estate sales.
    
- Occasional appraisal fairs.

## Secrets

- Ricard occasionally purchases items without asking too many questions about their origins.
    
- The vault contains several artifacts whose ownership is disputed.
    
- Certain high-value customers receive access to inventory not displayed publicly.
    

## History

- Established shortly after Brightshore's trade boom.
    
- Expanded alongside the growth of [[The Golden Sands]].
    
- Has become one of the city's most respected luxury merchants.
    

## Additional Notes

- Popular destination for adventurers looking to sell unusual treasures.
    
- Ricard can serve as a useful source of information regarding rare items and wealthy buyers.
    
- Security is discreet but effective.
