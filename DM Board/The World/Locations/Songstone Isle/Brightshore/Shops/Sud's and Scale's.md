# `=this.file.name`

## Overview

Sud's and Scale's is one of Brightshore's most infamous shops.

Run by the orc brothers Sud and Scale, the store somehow remains in business despite its terrible organization, questionable inventory management, and complete lack of professionalism.

The brothers are genuinely friendly but spectacularly incompetent. Prices are absurdly low, quality is wildly inconsistent, and customers can never be entirely certain what they will find on the shelves.

Despite all this, locals love the place.

Whether someone needs a rope, a shovel, a sword, a fishing net, a chair, or an object nobody can identify, Sud's and Scale's usually has one available somewhere beneath a pile of unrelated merchandise.
# Appearance

## Exterior Description

The building appears to have been repaired dozens of times using whatever materials happened to be available.

The front sign is visibly crooked and reads:

**"Sud's and Scale's General Goods & Stuff"**

Below that, in smaller paint:

**"Probably Cheapest In Brightshore"**

Several letters have fallen off over the years.

Crates, barrels, fishing nets, wagon wheels, and unidentified metal objects are stacked around the entrance.

No two windows match.

## Interior Layout

The interior resembles an organized disaster.

Narrow pathways weave through mountains of merchandise.

Shelves lean precariously.

Items are grouped according to logic understood only by the brothers.

Customers frequently discover products the owners forgot they possessed.

## Displays, Shelves, Counters

- Weapon rack mixed with kitchen utensils.
- Tool shelves containing several fish.
- Bargain bins labeled "Useful Stuff."
- Mystery crates for sale.
- Counter assembled from three different tables.

# Owner & Staff

## Sud

The older brother.

Huge, cheerful, and incredibly strong.

Sud handles heavy lifting, repairs, and customer service.

He often forgets prices halfway through transactions.

### Personality

- Friendly
- Honest
- Easily distracted
- Surprisingly wise on occasion

---

## Scale

The younger brother.

Slightly smarter than Sud, though not by much.

Responsible for inventory and bookkeeping.

Unfortunately, he cannot count particularly well.

### Personality

- Confident
- Creative
- Impulsive
- Frequently wrong

---

## Crate

A goblin employee hired years ago.

Nobody remembers exactly when.

Crate effectively runs the business while the brothers think they're in charge.

### Personality

- Sarcastic
- Practical
- Secretly exhausted

---

## Hidden Agendas, Debts, or Secrets

- Neither brother realizes the shop is profitable because Crate secretly handles finances.
