# Flububs' Curiosities

## Overview

A cramped and cluttered magic in the underbelly of  Brightshore's harbor. It is located under a bridge connecting two sections of the Waterfront and is accessible via an old stone staircase leading to the water grates under said bridge.  The shop is operated by Flububs, a grotesque gargoyle resembling a fish-man with bulging eyes and stone scales. Though not particularly intelligent, he enthusiastically attempts to sell the belongings of his former master, an aspiring wizard whose latest experiment ended very badly.

Most locals consider the shop eccentric rather than dangerous.

## Appearance

- **Exterior:** A shabbily put together storefront consisting of a lopsided wooden sign and some posts keeping it up. 
    
- **Interior:** Crowded with shelves, crates, and piles of magical junk.
    
- **Displays:** Random magical objects stacked wherever space can be found.
    
- **Lighting:** Not much, just the glow of magical bits and bobs. 
    
- **Smells:** Saltwater, dust, and burnt ozone.
    
- **Sounds:** Creaking stone, bubbling liquids, and Flububs talking to himself.
    

## Owner & Staff

### Flububs

- Animated gargoyle and sole proprietor.
    
- Friendly, talkative, and easily distracted.
    
- Struggles with counting coin.
    
- Genuinely believes he is a successful businessman.
    
- Secretly contains fragments of his creator's memories.
    
- Occasionally remembers things he shouldn't know.
    

### Staff

- None.
    
- Flububs occasionally attempts to hire customers.

## Secrets

- Flububs was created during a failed gargoyle creation ritual.
    
- His creator partially merged with the gargoyle rather than fully transferring.
    
- Several magical items still carry unstable enchantments.
    

## History

An aspiring magician attempted to create a gargoyle. 

The experiment failed catastrophically.

The wizard died, the gargoyle awoke, and Flububs inherited the shop.

Most people assume he's simply an unusual magical construct and don't ask too many questions.

## Additional Notes

- Flububs refers to everyone as "bububs."
    
- Has an irrational fear of pigeons.
    
- Frequently forgets prices.
    
- Sometimes offers completely unrelated items when asked for something specific.
    
- Surprisingly difficult to deceive despite appearing foolish.
    
- Excellent comic relief NPC.
