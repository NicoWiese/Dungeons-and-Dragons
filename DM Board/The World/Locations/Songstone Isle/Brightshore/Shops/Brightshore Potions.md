# Brightshore Potions

## Overview

Brightshore Potions is one of the most prestigious alchemical suppliers in the city. Catering primarily to wealthy merchants, nobles, ship captains, and visiting scholars, the shop offers both practical remedies and luxury concoctions imported from across Yá'rael.

Its affiliation with [[The Golden Sands]] ensures a steady supply of rare ingredients and exotic brews, though many locals joke that the prices are as potent as the potions themselves.

## Appearance

### Exterior Description

A three-story stone building overlooking one of Brightshore's wealthier market streets. Large glass windows display colorful potions and carefully arranged alchemical curiosities.

Polished brass signage hangs above the entrance, bearing a golden flask encircled by waves.

### Interior Layout

The ground floor serves as the main showroom, while upper floors contain offices, workshops, and storage.

Everything is meticulously organized and spotlessly clean.

### Displays, Shelves, Counters

- Glass cabinets displaying premium potions.
    
- Locked cases containing rare ingredients.
    
- Tall shelves lined with labeled bottles.
    
- Polished oak service counter.
    

### Lighting, Smells, Sounds

- Warm magical lanterns.
    
- Faint scents of herbs, flowers, and alcohol.
    
- Quiet conversations.
    
- Bubbling from distant brewing rooms.
    

## Owner & Staff

### [[Fizzwick Goldleaf]]

**Owner**

A wealthy gnome alchemist whose skill is exceeded only by his ego.

Fizzwick prides himself on serving "discerning clientele" and has little patience for customers who cannot pronounce the names of his products.

#### Personality

- Arrogant but competent.
    
- Extremely knowledgeable.
    
- Loves correcting people.
    
- Obsessed with appearances.
    

### [[Mira Copperpot]]

**Senior Brewer**

A practical dwarf who actually keeps the business running.

### [[Jorren Vale]]

**Apprentice**

A nervous human apprentice prone to mixing up labels.

### Secrets

- Fizzwick occasionally acquires ingredients without asking too many questions about their origins.
    
- The Golden Sands uses the shop to discreetly source alchemical supplies for expeditions.

## History

Founded approximately twenty years ago by [[Fizzwick Goldleaf]] after securing investment from [[The Golden Sands]].

The shop quickly became one of Brightshore's most successful alchemical businesses thanks to exclusive trade agreements and access to imported ingredients.

Today it serves as the unofficial center of alchemical commerce within the city.

## Additional Notes

- Fizzwick remembers every customer who has ever owed him money.
    
- The shop maintains a strict "no uncorking potions indoors" policy.
    
- Apprentices are required to wear brightly colored gloves after a series of unfortunate accidents.
    
- Wealthy clients often treat the establishment as much as a social club as a place of business.
