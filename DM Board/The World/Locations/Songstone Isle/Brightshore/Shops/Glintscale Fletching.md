# `=this.file.name`

## Overview

Glintscale Fletching is the premier archery supplier in Brightshore, catering primarily to wealthy mercenaries, merchant captains, noble hunters, and private guards. While common adventurers can purchase equipment here, the shop's reputation has been built upon supplying entire retinues with high-quality bows, arrows, and custom-crafted equipment.

Its products are considered some of the finest available anywhere on the Singing Stone Isles.

## Appearance

### Exterior Description

The shop occupies a large corner building overlooking one of Brightshore's merchant avenues. Its painted sign depicts a silver fish scale pierced by an arrow.

Large display windows showcase ornate bows and hunting trophies, while colorful pennants flutter from the upper floors.

### Interior Layout

The main showroom is spacious and well-organized, designed to impress wealthy clients.

Various sections display bows, ammunition, hunting gear, and custom commissions.

### Displays, Shelves, Counters

- Wall-mounted longbows and recurve bows.
    
- Glass cases containing specialty arrows.
    
- Decorative hunting trophies.
    
- Finished commissions displayed behind the main counter.
    

### Lighting, Smells, Sounds

- Warm lantern light.
    
- The scent of cedar, varnish, and leather.
    
- The occasional twang of bowstrings being tested.
    
- Quiet conversation between merchants and mercenaries.
    

## Owner & Staff

### Corvin Ashbow

Owner and master fletcher.

A former military quartermaster who served aboard merchant escorts before establishing his own business in Brightshore.

**Personality:**

- Professional.
    
- Detail-oriented.
    
- Proud of his craftsmanship.
    
- Values quality over profit.
    

### Mira Ashbow

Corvin's daughter and lead apprentice.

Handles many of the custom commissions and increasingly manages daily operations.

### Tellen Reed

Senior craftsman specializing in crossbows.

### Harven Pike

Former mercenary employed as security and occasional consultant.

### Secrets

## History

Founded nearly twenty years ago by Corvin Ashbow after retiring from maritime service.

What began as a small workshop supplying local hunters eventually grew into Brightshore's most respected fletching business. Through consistent quality and reliable service, Glintscale Fletching became the preferred supplier for merchant fleets, noble expeditions, and professional mercenary companies.

Today it is considered a symbol of quality craftsmanship throughout the region.

## Additional Notes

- During [[The Rainbow Tide]], limited-edition arrows become highly sought-after collector's items.
    
- Corvin can identify most arrows by craftsmanship alone.
    
- Adventurers seeking quality ranged equipment are frequently directed here.
    
- The shop is considered neutral ground among many of Brightshore's rival factions.
