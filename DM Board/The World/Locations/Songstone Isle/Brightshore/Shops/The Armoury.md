# `=this.file.name`

## Overview

[[The Armoury]] proudly advertises itself as:

> _"Brightshore's Finest Purveyor of Quality Goods."_

Most locals would replace the word _quality_ with _expensive._

Located in one of the wealthiest districts of Brightshore, the shop caters primarily to wealthy merchants, visiting nobles, successful adventurers, and anyone attempting to appear wealthier than they actually are.

The truth behind its success is an open secret among the city's merchants: much of the equipment sold here is no better than what can be purchased elsewhere for half the price.

What customers are truly paying for is prestige.

Every item bears the shop's golden seal, allowing buyers to signal wealth, status, and connections to the Golden Sands.

The shop thrives because many customers care more about appearances than practicality.

---

# Appearance

## Exterior Description

The building is an immaculate three-story structure of polished white stone and imported hardwoods.

Golden banners bearing the sigil of the Golden Sands hang from every balcony.

Large glass windows display gleaming weapons, armor, and luxury goods arranged with theatrical precision.

Two uniformed guards stand outside at all hours.

## Interior Layout

The interior resembles a noble gallery more than a shop.

Wide aisles and spacious displays ensure every item receives attention.

Customers are encouraged to browse slowly while attendants follow discreetly behind.

## Displays, Shelves, and Counters

- Velvet-lined weapon displays.
- Decorative armor stands.
- Imported crystal cases.
- Gold-trimmed counters.
- Framed certificates of authenticity.

Many displays are worth more than the products sitting on them.

## Lighting, Smells, Sounds

### Lighting

- Crystal chandeliers.
- Magical globe lights.
- Bright, flattering illumination.

### Smells

- Expensive perfumes.
- Polished wood.
- Fresh flowers.

### Sounds

- Soft instrumental music.
- Quiet conversations.
- Gentle clinking of glassware.

# Owner & Staff

## Percival Goldmere

A wealthy human merchant appointed by the Golden Sands.

Percival is charming, eloquent, and deeply convinced that presentation is everything.

He firmly believes customers should feel important while being overcharged.

### Personality

- Polished
- Ambitious
- Manipulative
- Friendly when profitable

## Lucien Vale

Senior sales representative.

---

## Golden Sands Guards

Highly trained security personnel.

Protect the shop's image as much as its merchandise.

# History

## Founding Story

Established shortly after Brightshore's economic boom began.

The Golden Sands recognized a growing market among newly wealthy merchants and adventurers.

---

## Past Owners

The business has always been guild-owned.

Managers simply rotate as needed.

---

## Major Events

- Expanded twice during the [[Shadowshard Crystal]] boom.
- Survived several investigations into pricing practices.
- Became the preferred supplier for many noble households.

---

## Reputation Over Time

While mocked by common citizens, [[The Armoury]]The  remains one of the most profitable businesses in Brightshore.

As one dockworker famously remarked:

> "They'll sell ye a shovel for ten gold and convince ye it digs better because it's polished."

---

# Additional Notes

- Excellent place to satirize wealthy merchants and status culture.
- Players quickly realize most products are absurdly overpriced.
- Wealthy NPCs often gather here, making it useful for political intrigue.
- Golden Sands agents use the shop to identify potential allies, investors, and rivals.
- Characters who expose the pricing scheme gain popularity among common folk but make powerful enemies.
