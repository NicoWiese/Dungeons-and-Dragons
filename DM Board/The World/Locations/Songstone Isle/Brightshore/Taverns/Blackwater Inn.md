# `=this.file.name`

## Overview

Blackwater Inn sits just far enough from Brightshore’s busiest docks to avoid the constant noise, but close enough to serve as a first or last stop for travelers coming in by sea or road.

It is not flashy, not famous, and not particularly memorable at first glance.

That is exactly why many prefer it.

Merchants stopping briefly in Brightshore often choose Blackwater Inn when they want comfort without attention, and sailors use it as a quiet recovery point after long voyages before re-entering the city’s more chaotic districts.

Despite its modest reputation, the inn has become an unspoken neutral ground for those who prefer not to be seen making arrangements elsewhere.

---

# Appearance

## Exterior Description

A two-story stone building built from dark basalt blocks stained by sea spray and rain.

Wooden beams frame the upper floor, slightly weathered but well maintained.

A simple black sign swings above the entrance, painted with a faint silver outline of a river meeting the sea.

Lanterns are kept dim, even at night.

The overall impression is practical rather than inviting.

---

## Interior Layout

The interior is warm and orderly, with an emphasis on function over decoration.

The main hall is long and narrow, with a central hearth running almost the length of one wall.

Sturdy tables line the room, spaced to allow private conversation.

The upper floor contains guest rooms accessed via a creaking wooden staircase.

A rear corridor leads to stables and storage.

# Owner & Staff

## Ser Dain Morrick

A quiet, composed man of uncertain origin who runs the inn with disciplined efficiency.

Some believe he was once military or naval personnel.

He rarely speaks about his past.

### Personality

- Reserved
- Observant
- Controlled
- Polite but distant
- Unfazed by conflict

---

## Lila Brent

Head cook and caretaker.

Known for simple but consistently excellent meals.

Keeps the inn running smoothly during busy periods.

---

## Oren Saltford

Stablemaster and general handyman.

Knows most travelers by their horses before their names.

---

## “Quiet” Merek

Night attendant.

Specializes in late arrivals and discreet check-ins.

Has a reputation for appearing where he is needed without being seen entering.

## Hidden Agendas
