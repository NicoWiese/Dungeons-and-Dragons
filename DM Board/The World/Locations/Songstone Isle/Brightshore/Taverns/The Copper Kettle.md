# `=this.file.name`

## Overview

The Copper Kettle is one of Brightshore’s most understated high-end taverns, quietly operating in the shadow of louder, more glamorous establishments like The Pearl.

It does not advertise itself with grand domes or glittering façades. Instead, it relies on reputation—particularly among Iron Ledger officials, engineers, and serious merchants who value discretion over spectacle.

Deals made here tend to stick. Not because of magic or coercion, but because the people who meet under its roof understand the consequences of breaking their word in places like this.

Locals often say:

> “If it’s agreed in the Copper Kettle, it’s already half law.”

---

# Appearance

## Exterior Description

A sturdy, three-story building of copper-toned brick and dark timber beams.

The roof has been oxidized by sea air, giving it a greenish sheen in places.

A simple hanging sign shows a stylized copper kettle suspended over a flame.

No bright lights. No banners. No theatrics.

Just warmth spilling faintly from curtained windows.

---

## Interior Layout

The interior is warm, practical, and carefully maintained.

Heavy wooden beams support low ceilings on the ground floor, while upper levels open into quieter, more private chambers.

The layout subtly encourages conversation at lower volumes and slower pacing.

Tables are spaced intentionally far apart.

Private booths line the walls, partially shielded by heavy curtains.

# Owner & Staff

## Brannick Hollowmere

A former Iron Ledger contract negotiator who retired early from field work.

Brannick is calm, deliberate, and almost impossible to rush.

He speaks little—but listens constantly.

### Personality

- Calculated
- Diplomatic
- Observant
- Patient
- Unshakably calm

---

## Tessa Marrow

Head chef and logistical manager.

Known for precise timing and flawless service execution.

---

## Old Finn

Bartender and informal historian of Brightshore.

---

## Lysa Quill

Records clerk.
