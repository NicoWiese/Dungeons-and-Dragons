# `=this.file.name`
## Overview

Perched upon one of Brightshore's higher terraces overlooking both the harbor and the shimmering waters beyond, The Pearl is widely considered the most luxurious tavern and inn in the Singing Stone Isles.

Its brilliant white walls, smooth curves, and striking domed architecture have earned it its name, resembling a polished pearl resting upon the cliffs above the sea.

The establishment caters primarily to wealthy merchants, visiting dignitaries, successful adventurers, and influential guild representatives. Common sailors joke that a single night at The Pearl costs more than their monthly wages.

Whether this is true depends on the room.

The Pearl has become a symbol of status within Brightshore. Being seen there is often as important as staying there.

---

# Appearance

## Exterior Description

The Pearl stands atop a cliffside overlooking the harbor.

The building is composed of smooth white stone polished to an almost reflective finish.

Its architecture is unusual for Yá'rael, featuring rounded walls, curved terraces, and several white domes overlooking the sea.

At sunset the building glows golden and can be seen from nearly anywhere in the harbor.

Flowing white banners flutter between balconies.

Decorative lanterns line the pathways leading to the entrance.

A fountain carved in the shape of an open pearl shell sits before the main doors.

---

## Interior Layout

The interior is spacious, elegant, and immaculately maintained.

Open balconies overlook the sea.

Arched hallways connect dining areas, lounges, guest suites, and private meeting rooms.

The central dining hall rises through two stories beneath the largest dome.

Large windows provide uninterrupted views of the Singing Stones and the Shimmering Reef.

A wealthy human entrepreneur and former merchant captain.

Valeria built much of her fortune transporting luxury goods throughout Southern Yá'rael before investing in hospitality.

# Additional Notes

- This is the social heart of Brightshore's upper class.
- Excellent location for political intrigue, espionage, and networking.
- Players may initially view it as unattainable luxury before eventually gaining access through wealth or reputation.
- Valeria is an ideal recurring NPC because she can provide information without seeming omniscient.
- During the Rainbow Tide Festival, The Pearl becomes one of the busiest and most influential locations in the city.
- While everyone notices the building's beauty, experienced merchants know its true value lies in the conversations taking place inside.
