# `=this.file.name`

## Overview

The Broken Anchor is one of Brightshore’s roughest and cheapest taverns, located closest to the docks where cargo ships unload and sailors spend their wages before the tide changes.

It is loud, overcrowded, and perpetually damp with seawater, spilled alcohol, and dockside mud.

Sailors come here to drink, gamble, fight, and forget—usually in that order.

Despite its reputation, it is also one of the most honest places in the harbor.

What you see is what you get.

Most of the time.

---

# Appearance

## Exterior Description

A squat, weather-beaten wooden building wedged between warehouses and rope yards.

The sign is a faded painted anchor missing one arm.

Lanterns swing violently in the sea wind.

The lower walls are stained by salt and brine.

---

## Interior Layout

The inside is cramped and chaotic.

Long wooden tables are scarred by years of knives, mugs, and brawls.

The bar runs the length of one wall, reinforced with iron bands.

A narrow staircase leads to small, overcrowded rooms upstairs.

The cellar door is always locked—but never well watched.

# Owner & Staff

## Captain Rusk Hollow

A retired merchant sailor who lost his ship, The Broken Anchor, during a storm decades ago.

He now runs the tavern that was named after his lost vessel.

Gruff, weathered, and surprisingly observant.

### Personality

- Blunt
- Practical
- Tired
- Fair (when sober)
- Dangerous when provoked

---

## Nella Grimes

Head bartender.

Keeps order with a glare and a heavy wooden club behind the counter.

Knows every regular by name—and debt.

---

## “Two-Tooth” Jory

Bouncer and occasional information broker.

Missing several teeth from various dockside incidents.

Surprisingly good at reading people.

---

## Hidden Agendas

- Rusk quietly tracks missing sailors and ship disappearances.
- Nella maintains an unofficial debt ledger for dockworkers.
- Some staff occasionally report to unnamed shipping interests.

# History

## Founding Story

Built around a wrecked ship salvaged after a major storm decades ago.

The ship’s name, _The Broken Anchor_, was kept as a tribute.

---

## Past Owners

Always operated by Captain Rusk Hollow.

---

## Major Events

- Multiple dockside riots.
- Several unexplained ship disappearances.
- Temporary closure after a harbor fire.

---

## Reputation Over Time

The Broken Anchor has remained a constant fixture of dockside life.

Sailors trust it not because it is safe—but because it is predictable.

# Additional Notes

- Perfect low-level hub for dockside quests and smuggling arcs.
- Strong contrast to The Pearl and The Copper Kettle.
- Ideal place to seed horror or mystery beneath Brightshore.
- Rusk can become a major recurring NPC tied to maritime disappearances.
- The tavern subtly hints at larger underwater/harbor cave mysteries connected to Shadowshard and the Singing Stones.
- Everything here feels ordinary… until it doesn’t.
