## Profile

The Golden Sands is one of the most powerful merchant guilds in Yá'rael, with holdings stretching across the southern and eastern regions of the world. Headquartered in [[Brightshore]], the guild has amassed enormous wealth through trade, shipping, mining, and aggressive commercial expansion.

Though respected for its efficiency and professionalism, the guild has earned a reputation for placing profit above all else. To the Golden Sands, morality is secondary to prosperity, and any obstacle can be overcome if the price is right.

## Story

Founded by a coalition of ambitious merchants, the Golden Sands rose to prominence by systematically absorbing competitors, securing exclusive trade agreements, and establishing monopolies over valuable resources.

Its latest venture is the exploitation of the newly discovered Shadowshard Crystal deposits on the Singing Stone Isles. Despite growing reports of disappearances, strange occurrences, and unrest among the local population, the guild continues to expand operations in pursuit of greater profits.

## Philosophy

The Golden Sands believes that wealth is the foundation of civilization and that those capable of generating prosperity deserve to rule.

Guild leaders view laws as tools that create stability for commerce. Rules are meant to be followed, contracts honored, and debts collected. Compassion, however, is considered a luxury that often interferes with progress.

Their motto is:

_"The winds of fortune favour us."_

## Relationships

The Golden Sands maintains relationships with governments, noble houses, mercenary companies, and trade organizations throughout Yá'rael.

Within [[Brightshore]], the guild is one of three dominant mercantile powers and holds three of the city's seven council seats. Its influence over local politics is considerable, allowing it to shape legislation, taxation, and trade policy to its advantage.

The guild is known to cooperate with almost anyone, provided the arrangement is profitable.

## Resources

The Golden Sands controls fleets of merchant vessels, warehouses, private docks, mining interests, caravan routes, and financial institutions throughout southern and eastern Yá'rael.

Its growing investments in [[Shadowshard Crystal]] extraction have made the guild wealthier than ever before.

## Methods

The guild prefers contracts, political influence, economic pressure, and strategic investments, over direct violence.

When force becomes necessary, however, it has no shortage of mercenaries, guards, bounty hunters, and adventurers willing to work for coin.

The Golden Sands rarely breaks the law. Instead, it writes the contracts, influences the lawmakers, and ensures the law works in its favour.

## Background

Over the last century, the Golden Sands has transformed from a modest trading consortium into one of the most influential commercial powers in the known world.

Its leadership sees the Singing Stone Isles as the next step in the guild's expansion. Whatever dangers may lie beneath the islands, they are viewed as acceptable risks compared to the fortunes waiting to be claimed.
