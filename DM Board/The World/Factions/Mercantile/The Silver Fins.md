## Profile

The Silver Fins are a merchant guild centred around maritime trade, exploration, and transportation. Though smaller than The Golden Sands, they are almost as entrenched as the Iron Ledger Consortium, and they possess one of the largest shipping fleets in Southern Yá'rael and maintain trade routes that stretch far beyond Brightshore.

Known for their adaptability and pragmatism, the Silver Fins thrive where others hesitate to venture. Their captains are often the first to chart new waters, and they don't shy away from deep and stormy seas. 

## Story

The Silver Fins began as an alliance of independent ship captains who pooled resources to protect one another from pirates, storms, and the monopolies of larger merchant organizations.

Over time, the alliance evolved into a powerful trade guild controlling a significant portion of maritime commerce throughout southern Yá'rael. While they lack the political influence of The Golden Sands and the industrial might of the Iron Ledger Consortium, their command of the sea ensures neither rival can ignore them.

## Philosophy

The Silver Fins believe that opportunity lies beyond the horizon.

Where others see dangerous waters, distant shores, or uncertain ventures, the guild sees possibility. Their leaders value adaptability, self-reliance, and practical decision-making above rigid ideology.

Their unofficial motto is:

_"Fortune Favors the Tide."_

## Relationships

The Silver Fins maintain working relationships with both The Golden Sands and the Iron Ledger Consortium, often acting as intermediaries between the two rival powers.

Many independent merchants prefer dealing with the Silver Fins, viewing them as less controlling than the Golden Sands and less bureaucratic than the Consortium.

The guild also maintains strong ties with sailors, explorers, navigators, harbor masters, and coastal communities throughout Yá'rael.

The guild is governed by a Council of Captains composed of the most successful fleet commanders and merchants within the organization. Decisions are often debated extensively, though once made they are generally respected by all members.

The Silver Fins hold two of Brightshore's seven council seats, placing them between the larger powers of the city and often making their support crucial in political disputes.

## Resources

The guild controls dozens of merchant vessels, exploration ships, coastal warehouses, and trade outposts throughout Yá'rael.

Their greatest asset is their information network. News, rumors, trade opportunities, and discoveries often reach the Silver Fins long before they become public knowledge.

## Methods

The Silver Fins prefer diplomacy, negotiation, and mutually beneficial agreements.

Unlike many commercial rivals, they rarely seek monopolies. Instead, they focus on establishing reliable trade routes and expanding into untapped markets.

When necessary, guild vessels are well-equipped to defend themselves against pirates, raiders, and hostile competitors.

## Background

For generations, the Silver Fins have connected distant ports and isolated communities through commerce. Their ships can be found in nearly every major harbour in southern Yá'rael, carrying goods, travellers, and information between regions.

Though often overshadowed by the wealth of The Golden Sands, their vast knowledge of the seas should not be thrown aside.
