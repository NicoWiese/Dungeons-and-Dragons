# The Glintscale Grab

A frantic fishing competition held from the docks.

### Rules

Participants are given a simple rod and have 3 rounds to catch as many fish as possible.

Each round, make a **Wisdom (Survival)** check.

|Check Result|Outcome|
|---|---|
|5 or less|No catch|
|6-10|1 point|
|11-15|2 points|
|16-20|3 points|
|21+|5 points|

At the end of 3 rounds, total the points.

### Special Fish

Roll 1d20 after each successful catch.

|d20|Fish|
|---|---|
|1-12|Common fish|
|13-17|Glintscale (+2 points)|
|18-19|Moonbream (+4 points)|
|20|Starlight Salmon (+8 points)|

### Prize

Fishing gear, gold, or local fame.

# Ribbon Runner Regatta

Small sailboats race through colorful fish-filled waters.

### Rules

First to earn 10 Race Points wins.

Each round the pilot chooses:

- Push Speed
- Maintain Course
- Risky Maneuver

Then make:

**Dexterity (Water Vehicles)** or **Dexterity (Acrobatics)**

|Result|Race Points|
|---|---|
|10-14|1|
|15-19|2|
|20+|3|

### Hazards

Each round roll 1d6.

|Roll|Hazard|
|---|---|
|1|Floating driftwood|
|2|Sudden current|
|3|School of fish|
|4|Rival boat splashes you|
|5-6|Clear waters|

Hazards require an additional DC 13 check or lose 1 Race Point.

# Petalfish Net Toss

A carnival-style game.

Players throw weighted nets over wooden fish targets.

### Rules

Make a ranged attack using Dexterity.

|Roll|Result|
|---|---|
|Miss AC 10|No fish|
|Hit AC 10|Small prize|
|Hit AC 15|Medium prize|
|Hit AC 20|Large prize|

Natural 20 wins a rare festival token.

# Songstone Listening Contest

Contestants sit among the ancient stones and try to hear the songs hidden beneath the waves.

### Rules

Three rounds.

Make either:

- Wisdom (Perception)
- Intelligence (History)
- Intelligence (Arcana)

Each successful check earns a clue.

### Success Levels

|Total Successes|Reward|
|---|---|
|1|Small trinket|
|2|Festival charm|
|3|Secret local rumor|
|4+|Hidden treasure map fragment|

Great for handing players actual clues to future adventures.

# The Splinterflint Dash

Fish occasionally leap across shallow channels.

Contestants race across floating platforms trying to catch one.

### Rules

First to 5 Catch Points wins.

Each round:

1. Make a Dexterity (Acrobatics) check.
2. Make a Dexterity check to grab the fish.

|Check|Result|
|---|---|
|Fail|Fall in water|
|Success|Gain 1 Catch Point|
|Success by 5+|Gain 2 Catch Points|

# Merchant's Madness

A loud bargaining competition popular among traders.

### Rules

Three rounds of negotiation.

Each round make one:

- Charisma (Persuasion)
- Charisma (Deception)
- Charisma (Intimidation)

Highest total wins.

### Festival Twist

Natural 20 earns an "impossible bargain" worth double points.

# The Whale's Call

A crowd favorite inspired by legends of the Songstone Whales.

### Rules

Contestants imitate whale songs.

Make a:

- Charisma (Performance)

|Result|Crowd Reaction|
|---|---|
|10-14|Polite applause|
|15-19|Loud cheers|
|20-24|Standing ovation|
|25+|Festival celebrity|

### Secret Event

If anyone rolls a natural 20, there is a 5% chance an actual distant whale call answers from the bay, causing an immediate crowd frenzy.

# Moonbream Lantern Race

A nighttime event.

Participants release floating lanterns and guide them through channels using long poles.

### Rules

First to earn 12 Progress Points wins.

Each round:

- Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) to steer.
- Wisdom (Perception) to spot currents.

Success earns 2 Progress Points.

Failure earns 1.

Critical failure causes the lantern to drift, losing 1 point.

# The Rainbow King's Catch (Grand Finale)

The most prestigious competition of the festival.

### Rules

Every participant gets a single cast.

Make a Wisdom (Survival) check.

|Result|Catch|
|---|---|
|1-5|Empty line|
|6-10|Common fish|
|11-15|Glintscale|
|16-20|Moonbream|
|21-25|Starlight Salmon|
|26+|Legendary Catch|

The winner is crowned **Rainbow King** or **Rainbow Queen** until the next festival and gains advantage on Charisma checks made with locals in Brightshore for the remainder of the celebration.

These games also make excellent covers for adventure hooks: smugglers can operate during the regatta, cultists can hide clues in the Listening Contest, and a real Songstone Whale appearance can signal that something unusual is happening beneath the Bay of Song.
