## Description

The fish native to the [[Singing Stone Isles]] gather within the [[Bay of Song]] in a phenomenon known as **The Rainbow Tide**. Over the course of 3 days, millions of fish enter the bay, their scales reflecting sunlight in brilliant displays of colour visible for miles.

Among the migrating species are [[Glintscales]], [[Moonbream]], [[Splinterflint]], [[Petalfish]], [[Starlight Salmon]], [[Ribbons of the Sea]], and, on rare occasions, the legendary [[Songstone Whales]]. The waters become so densely packed with marine life that portions of the bay appear to shimmer like liquid gemstones.

No scholar has ever fully explained why the migration occurs with such regularity, though countless theories exist.

## Location

The [[Bay of Song]], located at [[The Singing Stone Isles]].

## Causes and Effects

The Rainbow Tide is the single most important event in the regional calendar.

Fishermen take advantage of the abundance of fish, often earning enough during the event to sustain their families for weeks. Merchants arrive from across Yá'rael to buy rare catches, exotic goods, and local crafts. Inns, taverns, and markets overflow with visitors, causing the economy of [[Brightshore]] to surge.

The event has also become a major social and cultural gathering. Families travel great distances to witness the migration, performers fill the streets, and religious groups conduct ceremonies honoring the sea.

Common effects include:

- Massive increases in trade and tourism.
- Seasonal markets and merchant fairs.
- Fishing competitions and sailing races.
- Street performances and public celebrations.
- Temporary shortages of lodging throughout [[Brightshore]].
- Increased activity from thieves, smugglers, and opportunists seeking to profit from the crowds.

## Story

According to local legend, the spirit of the ocean rises from the depths once each month to greet her oldest friend, the sun. Their reunion fills the waters with light and colour, drawing every fish in the region to witness the celebration.

Many islanders believe each species participates in its own way. The shimmering scales of the [[Glintscales]] are said to carry the ocean's laughter, while the songs of the [[Songstone Whales]] are believed to be gifts exchanged between the two ancient spirits.

Other stories claim the fish are not celebrating at all, but answering a call from somewhere beneath the sea — gathering for reasons long forgotten by mortals.

Most dismiss such tales as sailor's superstition.

## Additional Details

- The first sighting of silver flashes beneath the water traditionally marks the start of the festival.
- Sightings of [[Songstone Whales]] are considered an exceptionally good omen.
- Marriages, business deals, and political announcements are often timed to coincide with the celebrations.
- Scholars frequently visit the islands to study the migration, though no consensus exists regarding its true cause.
- Some sailors claim the waters hum softly during the height of the Rainbow Tide, particularly near the ancient stone formations that gave the islands their name.
