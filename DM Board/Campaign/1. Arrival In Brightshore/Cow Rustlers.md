# Template – Storyline

## Overview
- The adventurers meet Old Bill, a cattle farmer that lost his beloved [[Friya]], an award winning Sea Bison. She disappeared from right in front of him, leaving no clue or trace behind!

## Player Hooks
- Old Bill is drowning his sorrows, and starts singing about poor lost [[Friya]]. Seems very depressed. 
- [[Nicole's Character]] hears he lost a cow, wants to investigate.

## Central Conflict
- [[Old Bill]]'s Sea Cows are being stolen. They were stolen by a dragon, but he installed a ballista to fend her off. Not long after, his cows started disappearing. He's a non-commercial farm, so the Cows' disappearance is bankrupting him, and nobody is willing to help since he can't pay. [[Friya]], his most prized Sea Cow, not to mention his last, was recently stolen. 
- The main antagonists are [[Crookedtooth]]and [[Toamu, Shepardsbane]].
- If the adventurers don't do anything, Old Bill will keep coming to [[The Pearl]] and drinking his sorrows away. Eventually he'll stop coming, and the adventurers an find him at [[Weathertop Farm]], dead, having killed himself. 

## Storyline Structure

- The adventurers find a lull in the festivities at the local tavern where an old man is singing a song about his lost cow, [[Friya]]. 
- They can offer to help find her, and will travel to [[Weathertop Farm]] to go look for clues. 
- They will find numerous footprints belonging to goblins, as well as a scrap of fabric that disappears when viewed from one side. 
- The tracks will lead the party into a nearby forest, where they can find remnants of a camp. Here they will hear sounds coming from the edge of the forest. They'll be ambushed if they pursue the noises, and after killing two of the goblins, the rest will flee. 
- The tracks will lead them to the edge of the cliff, where they will see pulleys and ropes alongside ladders leading down to a series of caves set into the stone. 
- In the caves, they'll find the [[Crookedtooth]] goblins, first sentries, then a main force as the adventurers head in deeper into the caves. Throughout the caves the adventurers will find scraps of fish, but no signs of any Sea Cows. 
- After the encounter with [[Crookedtooth]], the adventurers will learn that the goblins were stealing the cows for a dragon that had been terrorising local farms. 
- They can ask [[Crookedtooth]] about the location of the dragon's lair, but the information can also be gotten from any farmers, or from the soldiers. 
- The adventurers can then travel to the dragon's lair, which is set in a series of caves and tunnels in a waterlogged seaside cave. The lair can be accessed via boat, or via swimming. There is solid land a bit of a ways into the cave. 
- There will be various animal bones leading the way to a sandy cave, with a lone [[blue-dragon-wyrmling-xmm|Blue Dragon Wyrmling]] nestled on a pillar of stone about 10m up into the air. There are other similar pillars that are a bit lower, each housing either a pile of animal bones or an animal. [[Friya]] is trapped on one of those pillars. 
- Upon defeating [[Toamu, Shepardsbane]], the adventurers can take [[Friya]] back to [[Old Bill]]. 

## Background (DM Only)
[[Toamu, Shepardsbane]] is a [[blue-dragon-wyrmling-xmm|Blue Dragon Wyrmling]] that fled her desert home and took up on The [[Songstone Isle]], where she started preying on local cattle and sheep farmers.
