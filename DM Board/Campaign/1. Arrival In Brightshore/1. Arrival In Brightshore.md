# Template – Storyline

## Overview

The adventurers arrive at the [[Songstone Isle]], passing through [[The Kraken's Maw]] before docking at the bustling port of [[Brightshore]]. Preparations are underway for [[The Rainbow Tide]], a monthly festival celebrating the migration of brilliantly coloured fish through the harbor.

Representatives of [[The Golden Sands]] are recruiting adventurers and mercenaries to assist with operations at [[Shadowshard Mine]] near [[Hollowcove]]. The job appears straightforward and well-paid.

Beneath the celebrations, however, tensions are growing. Workers have gone missing, strange rumors circulate among sailors, and some locals whisper that the sea has become restless.

## Player Hooks

- Attend festivities during [[The Rainbow Tide]], talk to vendors, play festival games, etc. 
    
- Accept employment from [[The Golden Sands]], sign their contract. 
    
- Investigate strange rumours from sailors about a tentacled creature seen at night, accompanied by a sickly red glow coming from the far shore. 
    
- Witness the death of an injured dwarf from [[Hollowcove]].
    
- Become involved in political disputes between [[Brightshore]]'s merchant factions.
    

### Festival Incident

- During the celebrations, screams erupt from a nearby crowd.
    
- [[Tunsel Shimmerbeard]] staggers into view.
    
- Eyes completely black.
    
- Bleeding strange black blood.
    
- Mutters fragmented warnings:
    
    - "The mine..."
        
    - "Please....help"
- Dies shortly afterward.
    

Before questions can be answered:

- [[Farwen Evergreen]] arrives with members of [[The Gilded Masts]].
    
- The body is seized immediately.
    
- Witnesses are dispersed.
    
- Officials discourage further investigation.
    

Players may:

- Examine the body.
    
- Follow the soldiers.
    
- Question witnesses.
    
- Ignore the event entirely.

## Central Conflict

### Main Problem

Nobody understands why people connected to [[Hollowcove]] and [[Shadowshard Mine]] are disappearing.

### Opposition

- Unknown corruption beneath the islands.
    
- Political factions attempting to suppress information.
    
- Future antagonist operating through servants and vessels.
    

### If Players Do Nothing

- The mine continues operating.
    
- More workers disappear.
    
- The corruption spreads unnoticed.

- Arrive in Brightshore.
    
- Experience [[The Rainbow Tide]].
    
- Meet representatives from [[The Golden Sands]].
    
- Witness [[Tunsel Shimmerbeard]] collapse.
    
- Learn of opportunities at [[Shadowshard Mine]].
    

### Act 2 – Rising Action

Potential activities:

- Investigate Tunsel's death.
    
- Gather rumors in taverns.
    
- Speak to sailors arriving from [[Hollowcove]].
    
- Learn about local factions.
    
- Explore Brightshore.
    
- Uncover evidence that the authorities are hiding something.
    

Possible discoveries:

- Missing persons reports.
    
- Strange cargo arriving from Hollowcove.
    
- Similar symptoms in other victims.
    

### Act 3 – Climax

This session may not require a true climax.

Options:

- Confront Farwen.
    
- Infiltrate a warehouse storing confiscated evidence.
    
- Recover samples of black blood.
    
- Decide whether to pursue the mystery or simply take the mining contract.
    

### Act 4 – Resolution

- Accept passage to [[Hollowcove]].
    
- Gain allies or enemies in Brightshore.
    
- Leave with unanswered questions.
    
- Set sail toward [[Shadowshard Mine]].

### True History

- The corruption beneath the islands has existed for centuries.

- Victims are appearing faster than local authorities can manage.

- Establish Brightshore as a living city.
    
- Make The Golden Sands seem helpful but slightly self-interested.
    
- Foreshadow the black blood corruption.

- Leave more questions than answers.
