# Template – Storyline

## Overview

[[Marria Briarhand]], a local apothecary and potion-maker, has been unable to gather ingredients from one of her favorite groves due to the presence of a ferocious owlbear. She offers the adventurers a reward to clear the beast out so she can resume harvesting.

As the party investigates, they discover the owlbear is behaving strangely and that the grove itself has begun to change. What appears to be a simple monster hunt may actually be the first sign of a deeper corruption spreading through the wilderness.

**Tone & Themes:** Adventure, Exploration, Nature Corruption, Moral Choices

---

## Player Hooks

- [[Marria Briarhand]] offers gold and potions in exchange for dealing with the owlbear.
    
- Local hunters complain about increased animal aggression near the grove.
    
- The party needs rare herbs only found within the grove.
    
- Rumors suggest strange lights and noises have been seen among the trees.
    

### Immediate Goal

- Eliminate or remove the owlbear preventing access to the grove.
    

---

## Key People, Places, and Things

### NPCs

- [[Marria Briarhand]]
    
    - Role: Quest giver and potion merchant.
        
    - Motivation: Regain access to valuable herbs and ingredients.
        
    - Secrets: Knows the grove has changed recently but fears losing business if people become afraid of it.
        
    - Personality/quirks:
        
        - Cheerful and talkative.
            
        - Constantly gathering ingredients.
            
        - Can identify almost any plant on sight.
            
- Corrupted Owlbear
    
    - Role: Apparent antagonist.
        
    - Motivation: Protect its territory and cubs.
        
    - Secrets: It is suffering from a corrupting influence rather than acting naturally.
        
    - Personality/quirks:
        
        - Unusually aggressive.
            
        - Appears injured or sick.
            
        - Displays moments of confusion.
            
- Local Hunter (Optional)
    
    - Role: Source of information.
        
    - Motivation: Keep nearby settlements safe.
        
    - Secrets: Has seen other animals behaving strangely.
        
    - Personality/quirks:
        
        - Distrustful of magic.
            
        - Loves telling exaggerated stories.
            

### Locations

- Briarhand's Apothecary
    
    - Description: Small shop filled with herbs, potions, and strange ingredients.
        
    - Notable Features:
        
        - Drying herbs hanging from every wall.
            
        - Shelves packed with colorful bottles.
            
    - Encounters/events:
        
        - Potion shopping.
            
        - Ingredient identification.
            
- The Corrupted Grove
    
    - Description: A once-peaceful woodland clearing now touched by an unnatural influence.
        
    - Notable Features:
        
        - Twisted vegetation.
            
        - Blackened roots.
            
        - Unnaturally silent wildlife.
            
    - Encounters/events:
        
        - Strange animal behavior.
            
        - Discovery of corruption.
            
- Owlbear Den
    
    - Description: Hidden nest deep within the grove.
        
    - Notable Features:
        
        - Signs of recent struggle.
            
        - Corrupted plant growth.
            
        - Evidence of cubs or prey.
            
    - Encounters/events:
        
        - Owlbear confrontation.
            
        - Clues to the true cause.
            

### Important Items / MacGuffins

- Corrupted Root Cluster
    
    - Description: A mass of dark roots pulsing with unnatural energy.
        
    - Purpose in story: Source of the grove's corruption.
        
    - Who wants it and why:
        
        - Marria for study.
            
        - Scholars interested in strange phenomena.
            
- Bloomshade Herb
    
    - Description: Rare medicinal plant found only within the grove.
        
    - Purpose in story: Valuable reward ingredient.
        
    - Who wants it and why:
        
        - Apothecaries and healers.

## Central Conflict

### Main Problem

- A dangerous owlbear is preventing access to a valuable grove.
    

### Opposition

- The corrupted owlbear.
    
- Corrupted wildlife.
    
- The spreading influence affecting the grove.
    

### If Players Do Nothing

- The corruption continues to spread.
    
- More wildlife becomes aggressive.
    
- The grove eventually becomes completely overrun.
    
- Valuable resources are lost.
    

---

## Storyline Structure

### Investigation

- Meet [[Marria Briarhand]].
    
- Learn about the owlbear attacks.
    
- Travel to the grove.
    

### Discovery

- Find evidence the owlbear is behaving unnaturally.
    
- Encounter corrupted plants and animals.
    
- Track the beast to its den.
    

### Revelation

- Discover the true source of the corruption.
    
- Realize the owlbear may be a victim rather than the cause.
    

### Resolution

- Kill the owlbear.
    
- Cure the corruption.
    
- Drive the creature away.
    
- Cleanse the grove.

## Background (DM Only)

### True History of Events

- The grove was once a healthy gathering site for local herbalists.
    
- A source of corruption recently entered the area.
    
- The owlbear was one of the first creatures affected.
    

### Hidden Motivations

- Marria genuinely wants the grove restored.
    
- The owlbear is acting from pain, fear, and corruption.
    
- The corruption may connect to larger events occurring across the islands.
    

### Timeline Before Players Arrive

Months Ago:

- Grove remains healthy.
    

Weeks Ago:

- Strange plant growth appears.
    

Days Ago:

- Owlbear attacks begin.
    

Present:

- Marria seeks outside help.

- Animals watching the party from the trees.
    
- Strange spores drifting through the grove.
    
- Trees bleeding dark sap.
    
- Unnatural silence falling over the forest.
    

### Side Quests

- Gather rare herbs for Marria.
    
- Rescue a trapped hunter.
    
- Investigate reports of another corrupted creature.
    

### Improvisation Notes

- Present the owlbear as a threat initially.
    
- Gradually reveal signs that it is suffering.
    
- Allow multiple solutions beyond simply killing it.
    

### Session-Specific Reminders

- Emphasize the contrast between the grove's former beauty and its current state.
    
- Make the owlbear sympathetic once players investigate.
    
- Leave clues connecting the corruption to larger island mysteries.
    
- Reward creative or compassionate solutions.
