# Main Storyline

## Overview
The adventurers receive an offer almost too good to refuse: 20 gold pieces to help protect the [[Shadowshard Mine]] from a few goblin raiders. 

The [[Shadowshard Mine]] is a newly established endeavour that aims to cash in on the mysterious [[Shadowshard Crystals]] recently discovered on the island. The local villagers from [[DM Board/The World/Locations/Hollowcove]] have been hired to help out as manual labour, and a garrison from the [[The Holy Empire of Al'Araan]]'s military was sent to oversee the operation. 

Not long after their arrival, the adventurers start to notice things not quite adding up. 

**Tone & Themes:** Mystery, cosmic horror, paranoia, moral ambiguity  

## Story Hooks
- **[[1. Too Good To Refuse]]** - The adventurers receive an offer almost too good to refuse: 20 gold pieces to help protect the [[Shadowshard Mine]] from a few goblin raiders. 
  
- **[[2. A Missing Mother]]** - The adventurers receive a plea for help from Rosie, a little dwarf girl from the nearby village. She says that her mom went missing, and nobody is helping her look.

- **[[3. Sudden Changes]]** - The garrison sent to watch over [[Shadowshard Mine]] say the villagers have been behaving oddly the last few weeks, ever since a dead [[Dead Drow]] was discovered at the mine entrance. 

**Personal Stakes:**

- The girl reminds a player of someone in their past. 
- A player has ties to the garrison or nearby region.
- Strange dreams of drowning or whispering voices affect one or more players. 

## Key People, Places, and Things

### NPCs
**Name:** [[Rosalind Shimmerlock]]

- **Role:** Important NPC
- **Quest Giver** : Yes
- **Motivation:** Needs help finding her mom. 
- **Secrets:** Has been told by the dark god that they can help find her mom if she lures more people to the island. 
- **Personality:** Nervous, serious, observant.

**Name:** [[Erkan Halvin]]

- **Role:** Garrison leader
- **Motivation:** Prevent “something unnatural” from spreading
- **Secrets:** Has seen a partial transformation firsthand
- **Personality:** Stern, exhausted, suspicious

**Name:** [[Thalos Darkbarrel]]

- **Role:** Cult leader posing as village tavernkeeper
- **Motivation:** Complete a summoning ritual
- **Secrets:** Already partially transformed
### Locations
**Name:** Hollowcove Village

- **Description:** Quiet fishing village with too-perfect routines
- **Notable Features:** Empty streets at night, strong smell of salt and rot
- **Encounters:** Villagers subtly watching the party

**Name:** The Old Chapel

- **Description:** Converted shrine near the cliffs
- **Notable Features:** Waterlogged altar, strange carvings
- **Encounters:** Secret meetings, hidden passage

**Name:** The Drowned Caverns

- **Description:** Tidal caves beneath the island
- **Notable Features:** Bioluminescent growths, deep chanting echoes
- **Encounters:** Ritual site, aquatic horrors

### Important Items / MacGuffins
**Name:** The Pearl of Calling

- **Description:** Pulsating, organic orb that hums faintly
- **Purpose:** Focus for summoning the eldritch entity
- **Who Wants It:** Cult (to complete ritual), Garrison (to destroy it)

## Central Conflict
The islanders are secretly part of a cult attempting to summon a deep-sea entity.

**Opposition:**

- Cult of the Drowned Choir
- Elder Thalos (primary antagonist)

**If Players Do Nothing:**  
The ritual succeeds, and the entity’s influence spreads to the mainland.

## Storyline Structure

### Act 1 – Introduction
- Party arrives on island
- Meets villagers and garrison
- Lysa disappears shortly after arrival

### Act 2 – Rising Action
- Investigating chapel, homes, shoreline
- Discover hidden symbols and rituals
- Garrison confrontation or uneasy alliance
- Lysa may have been taken… or joined willingly
- Some villagers begin transforming

### Act 3 – Climax
- Ritual in the Drowned Caverns

**Defining Challenge:**

- Stop the ritual **or** survive its partial success

**Boss Encounter:**

- Elder Thalos (mutated form)
- Possible appearance of the entity’s manifestation (not full form)

### Act 4 – Resolution
- Island either saved, corrupted, or lost

**Consequences:**

- Garrison survives or is wiped out
- Survivors spread the cult unknowingly

## Background (DM Only)

**Truth:**  
The cult has existed for generations, slowly preparing for this moment.

**Hidden Motivations:**

- The cult believes they will be “reborn” as something greater
- The entity is not a god—it is unknowable and indifferent

**Timeline:**

- Years ago: Artifact discovered
- Months ago: Ritual preparations begin
- Days ago: Garrison intervenes
- Now: Ritual nearing completion
