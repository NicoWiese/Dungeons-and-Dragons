# Template – Storyline

## Overview
- A little dwarven girl named [[Rosalind Shimmerlock]] and her mom, [[Jane Shimmerlock|Jane]], work at the tavern that the adventurers are staying at. A while after arriving, [[Jane Shimmerlock|Jane]] goes missing, and [[Rosalind Shimmerlock|Rosie]] starts staying with [[Thalos Darkbarrel]], [[The Drowned Dwarf]]'s tavernkeeper. She asks the adventurers for help looking, and along with the [[Hollowcove]] villagers, they search high and low for the missing woman. 
- After a while, the villagers become more and more secretive about [[Rosalind Shimmerlock|Rosalind]]'s mom's disappearance, and become less and less willing to help. [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] eventually asks the adventurers to stop asking questions, saying that [[Rosalind Shimmerlock]] has a new family now. 
- The adventurers keep asking around, and get more and more alienated by the people of [[Hollowcove]]. Following the clues will eventually lead them to [[The Drowned Dwarf]]'s basement, which is locked with a magical rune. 
- The rune can be unlocked by smearing it with black blood, which gets absorbed by the rune, causing it to glow and dispel (the same can be done with the run at on the other side). The adventurers then enter the basement, where they will find [[Jane Shimmerlock]], locked in a cage between all the ale barrels. The barrels, when inspected, are mostly empty. There are a few full ones that contain black blood, mixed with seaweed and dead fish. 
- Once the adventurers unlocks the cage they can try to speak to [[Jane]]  however she's too far gone by the time they reach her. The adventurers can take her with them, or kill her out of mercy. 
- If the adventurers do either of the above, [[Thalos Darkbarrel]] will become hostile, and attack them, along with a few of the patrons. If they chose to leave [[Jane Shimmerlock|Jane]] without doing anything, they can then confront [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] themselves. 
- [[Rosalind Shimmerlock]] is then sent to the garrison to stay with [[Erkan Halvin]], the garrison commander there.

## Storyline Structure

- The adventurers arrive in the isolated fishing village of [[Hollowcove]], where a dwarven woman named [[Jane Shimmerlock]] suddenly disappears, leaving behind her daughter, [[Rosalind Shimmerlock|Rosie]]. Rosie begs the adventurers to help find her mother, insisting she would never abandon her willingly.

- Rosie is taken in by [[Thalos Darkbarrel]], owner of [[The Drowned Dwarf]], a tavern where the adventurers quickly notice strange behavior among the villagers. Patrons sit silently for hours, barely eating or drinking, while watching the newcomers with unsettling attention.

- Though seemingly helpful, [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] constantly redirects the adventurers toward other villagers instead of helping himself. He spends most of his time tending the tavern and caring obsessively for a finely crafted dwarven kitchen knife named [[Eberhart]].

- As the investigation continues, the atmosphere in [[Hollowcove]] grows increasingly hostile. Villagers avoid conversation, windows close as the adventurers pass, and whispers spread that they should stop searching altogether.

- The adventurers discover strange runes carved onto locked doors around the tavern and eventually uncover a hidden cellar beneath [[The Drowned Dwarf]]. The seals can only be opened using a strange black substance found near the shoreline and throughout the tavern.

- Inside the cellar, the adventurers find barrels filled with foul mixtures of seawater, dead fish, and black blood — and imprisoned in the center is [[Jane Shimmerlock]]. Her body is covered in carved runes, her mind shattered beyond repair. She can only repeat the words:  _“He is coming.”_

- When the adventurers attempt to free or kill Jane, [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] and several villagers reveal their corruption and attack. Black veins spread beneath their skin as they speak of an ancient being beneath the sea that saved the village from famine in exchange for devotion.

- Following the confrontation, [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] escapes, and the fate of [[Hollowcove]] is left to the adventurers. They may attempt to cleanse the corruption, destroy the tavern, expose the villagers, or leave whatever ancient horror lurks beneath the ocean undisturbed.

- [[Rosalind Shimmerlock|Rosie]] survives the ordeal, though forever scarred by the loss of her mother and the truth hidden beneath her village. Even afterward, rumors persist that something beneath the tides is still waiting.

## Background (DM Only)
- Several months ago, [[Hollowcove]] suffered its worst fishing season in living memory. Fisherman weren't catching anything, crops were dying etc. 
- A local tavernkeeper named [[Thalos Darkbarrel]] found old manuscripts hidden behind a false wall in the basement of [[The Drowned Dwarf]]. Hidden behind the stone, he found fragmented writings describing “the tide beneath the world” and rituals involving black blood drawn from something deep beneath the sea, along with an ancient dagger, referred to as [[The Mark of the Vessel]]. Soon after the discovery, [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] starts hearing voices in his head. 
- One night, after following the strange whispers along the shoreline caves beneath the village, [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] discovered a submerged chamber containing an ancient black ichor slowly leaking from cracks in the stone. The substance appeared alive, reacting to touch and whispering to him. 
- The black blood promised to restore wealth and prosperity to the island, but for a price. [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] would have to offer sacrifices to the entity. Initially, the sacrifices were small creatures, like fish and rats, and eventually it became goblins and the like. Fish returned in enormous quantities. Storms passed harmlessly around the village. Hunger disappeared. Illness became rare.
- But the blessing came with a cost. Those exposed to the rituals and black blood slowly lost pieces of themselves. They became emotionally numb, secretive, and increasingly devoted to the entity beneath the sea. Their bodies began changing — rotting flesh, and darkened veins.
- [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] became the unwilling leader of this transformation. At first he resisted it, but eventually convinced himself that the corruption was necessary for the village’s survival.
- The runes carved into the villagers act as both marks of devotion and conduits for the entity’s influence. Using [[The Mark of the Vessel]],  [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] personally carved the symbols into the villagers during secret midnight rituals in the tavern cellar.
- [[Jane Shimmerlock]] discovered the truth after following her husband’s fishing crew to the shoreline caves late at night. She witnessed the rituals beneath the tavern and threatened to expose the entire village to the nearby garrison.
- Rather than kill her immediately, [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] imprisoned [[Jane Shimmerlock|Jane]] beneath [[The Drowned Dwarf]]. The villagers believed the black blood could eventually “convert” her completely, just as it had converted them.
- Over the last two days, however, [[Jane Shimmerlock|Jane]]’s condition rapidly deteriorated. Instead of becoming obedient, she began hearing the voice beneath the sea more clearly than the others ever could. Whatever she heard shattered her mind completely.
- Her repeated words — _“He is coming”_ — are not madness alone. They are a warning. The entity beneath the ocean is awakening, and the corruption spreading through [[Hollowcove]] is only the beginning.
