# Template – Storyline

## Overview
- A little dwarven girl named [[Rosalind Shimmerlock]] and her mom, [[Jane Shimmerlock|Jane]], work at the tavern that the adventurers are staying at. A while after arriving, [[Jane Shimmerlock|Jane]] goes missing, and [[Rosalind Shimmerlock|Rosie]] starts staying with [[Thalos Darkbarrel]], [[The Drunken Miner]]'s tavernkeeper. She asks the adventurers for help looking, and along with the [[Hollowcove]] villagers, they search high and low for the missing woman. 
- After a while, the villagers become more and more secretive about [[Rosalind Shimmerlock|Rosalind]]'s mom's disappearance, and become less and less willing to help. [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] eventually asks the adventurers to stop asking questions, saying that [[Rosalind Shimmerlock]] has a new family now. 
- The adventurers keep asking around, and get more and more alienated by the people of [[Hollowcove]]. Following the clues will eventually lead them to [[The Drunken Miner]]'s basement, which is locked with a magical rune. 
- The rune can be unlocked by smearing it with black blood, which gets absorbed by the rune, causing it to glow and dispel (the same can be done with the run at on the other side). The adventurers then enter the basement, where they will find [[Jane Shimmerlock]], locked in a cage between all the ale barrels. The barrels, when inspected, are mostly empty. There are a few full ones that contain black blood, mixed with seaweed and dead fish. 
- Once the adventurers unlocks the cage they can try to speak to [[Jane]]  however she's too far gone by the time they reach her. The adventurers can take her with them, or kill her out of mercy. 
- If the adventurers do either of the above, [[Thalos Darkbarrel]] will become hostile, and attack them, along with a few of the patrons. If they chose to leave [[Jane Shimmerlock|Jane]] without doing anything, they can then confront [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] themselves. 
- [[Rosalind Shimmerlock]] is then sent to the garrison to stay with [[Erkan Halvin]], the garrison commander there.

## Storyline Structure

### Act 1 – Introduction
- The adventurers arrive in the isolated fishing village of [[Hollowcove]], where a dwarven woman named [[Jane Shimmerlock]] and her daughter, [[Rosalind Shimmerlock]], are working at on of the local taverns, [[The Drunken Miner]]. After a few days, [[Jane Shimmerlock|Jane]] goes missing, and [[Rosalind Shimmerlock|Rosie]] is taken in by [[Thalos Darkbarrel]], owner of [[The Drunken Miner]], who had been a friend of her family.
- [[Rosalind Shimmerlock|Rosalind]] desperately asks the adventurers to help find her mother. Though frightened, she insists her mother would never abandon her willingly.
- The villagers initially appear cooperative and sympathetic. Fishermen, laborers, and dock workers offer small leads and stories about strange noises near the coast during the night of the disappearance.
- Throughout the investigation, [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] repeatedly directs the adventurers toward other villagers who “might know more,” yet never personally joins the search. He always seems busy tending the tavern, cleaning glasses, or preparing food.
- During meals at [[The Drunken Miner]], the adventurers may notice that [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] possesses an unusually fine kitchen knife named [[Eberhart]], a beautifully crafted dwarven blade he proudly explains was forged together with his grandfather. He treats the knife with near-religious care.
- The tavern itself feels increasingly unnatural. The ale supplies are running low, yet none of the patrons seem concerned. Many barely eat or drink at all. Instead, they sit in silence, quietly watching the adventurers from dark corners of the room.
- A locked cellar door behind the bar bears a small runic seal carved near the bottom of the frame. Most villagers dismiss it as an old dwarven lock. A matching rune can later be found carved onto an old wooden door hidden behind the tavern near the shoreline.
- The adventurers eventually discover that [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] has started to follow a disturbingly precise routine. Every night at exactly 03h00 he descends into the basement and does not emerge again until 07h00 the following day. No one in the tavern questions this behaviour.

### Act 2 – Rising Action
- As the adventurers continue asking questions, the mood within [[Hollowcove]] changes dramatically. Villagers become distant and evasive, conversations stop when the adventurers enter rooms, and windows close whenever they walk through the streets.
- [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] eventually asks the adventurers to stop searching altogether, insisting that [[Rosalind Shimmerlock|Rosalind]] “has a new family now.” His tone remains calm, but there is unmistakable menace beneath his words.
- Over time, [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] himself begins changing physically. He avoids eye contact entirely, keeps his back turned whenever possible, and often stares silently at the floor. A foul odor of dead fish and rotting flesh follows him wherever he goes.
- The tavern patrons begin wearing heavy gloves and long-sleeved clothing regardless of the weather. They react aggressively if anyone touches them. Should their gloves be removed, the adventurers discover carved runes etched into the backs of their hands — symbols nearly identical to the seal on the cellar door.
- Investigating the rear entrance behind the tavern reveals another matching rune carved into an old weather-beaten wooden door near the shore. Black residue stains the ground nearby, leading back toward [[The Drunken Miner]].
- Clues eventually lead the adventurers to experiment with the strange black substance found around the tavern. When smeared across the rune seals, the black blood is absorbed instantly, causing the symbols to glow faintly before dissolving and opening the doors.
- Beneath the tavern lies a hidden cellar filled with ale barrels. Most are empty, though several still contain horrifying mixtures of black blood, seawater, seaweed, and dead fish floating inside like grotesque brewing ingredients.
- Locked within a rusted cage in the center of the room is [[Jane Shimmerlock]]. Her body is covered in carved runes identical to those found throughout the village. Black tears stream constantly from her eyes, and despite still being alive, her body and mind are clearly ruined beyond recovery.

### Act 3 – Climax
- Once freed from her cage, [[Jane Shimmerlock|Jane]] proves incapable of meaningful conversation. Her mind has been shattered by whatever ritual or corruption was inflicted upon her. Between sobs and moments of panic, she repeats only three words:
- _“He is coming.”_
- The adventurers may attempt to rescue her despite her condition, though it quickly becomes clear she will never fully recover. Alternatively, they may decide to end her suffering out of mercy.
- Any attempt to remove or kill [[Jane Shimmerlock|Jane]] immediately provokes [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] and several tavern patrons into violence. Their transformation is no longer subtle — blackened veins crawl beneath their skin, their movements become unnatural, and the smell of the sea fills the room.
- During the confrontation, [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] speaks of a being beneath the ocean that “blessed” the village during a season of famine and dying catches. The black blood, according to him, was a gift that ensured [[Hollowcove]] would survive.
- If the adventurers instead leave [[Jane Shimmerlock|Jane]] alive in the cellar, they may confront [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] directly before violence erupts. He insists the transformation spreading through the village is necessary, inevitable, and nearly complete.

### Act 4 – Resolution
- Following the conflict, the fate of [[Hollowcove]] rests entirely in the adventurers’ hands. They may expose the villagers, cleanse the corruption, burn the tavern to the ground, or attempt to contain whatever darkness lies beneath the settlement.
- [[Rosalind Shimmerlock]] is ultimately placed into the care of [[Erkan Halvin]], commander of the local garrison. Though safe, she remains traumatized by the loss of her mother and the horrifying truth hidden beneath her home village.
- Even after the events conclude, the villagers whisper that the tide still rises at night — and somewhere beneath the black ocean, something ancient is still waiting.

## Background (DM Only)
- Several months ago, [[Hollowcove]] suffered its worst fishing season in living memory. Nets returned empty, boats vanished in heavy fog, and starvation slowly began creeping through the village.
- Desperate to save his tavern and his people, [[Thalos Darkbarrel]] began searching through old dwarven records left behind in the cellar of [[The Drunken Miner]]. Hidden among them were fragmented writings describing “the tide beneath the world” and rituals involving black blood drawn from something deep beneath the sea.
- One night, after following strange whispers along the shoreline caves beneath the village, [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] discovered a submerged chamber containing an ancient black ichor slowly leaking from cracks in the stone. The substance appeared alive, reacting to touch and whispering into the minds of those nearby.
- The black blood granted impossible things at first. Fish returned in enormous quantities. Storms passed harmlessly around the village. Hunger disappeared. Illness became rare.
- But the blessing came with a cost. Those exposed to the blood slowly lost pieces of themselves. They became emotionally numb, secretive, and increasingly devoted to the entity beneath the sea. Their bodies began changing — rotting flesh, darkened veins, and strange runes appearing across their skin.
- [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] became the unwilling leader of this transformation. At first he resisted it, but eventually convinced himself that the corruption was necessary for the village’s survival.
- The runes carved into the villagers act as both marks of devotion and conduits for the entity’s influence. Using [[Eberhart]], the ancestral knife forged with his grandfather, [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] personally carved the symbols into the villagers during secret midnight rituals in the tavern cellar.
- [[Jane Shimmerlock]] discovered the truth after following her husband’s fishing crew to the shoreline caves late at night. She witnessed the rituals beneath the tavern and threatened to expose the entire village to the nearby garrison.
- Rather than kill her immediately, [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] imprisoned [[Jane Shimmerlock|Jane]] beneath [[The Drunken Miner]]. The villagers believed the black blood could eventually “convert” her completely, just as it had converted them.
- Over the last two days, however, [[Jane Shimmerlock|Jane]]’s condition rapidly deteriorated. Instead of becoming obedient, she began hearing the voice beneath the sea more clearly than the others ever could. Whatever she heard shattered her mind completely.
- Her repeated words — _“He is coming”_ — are not madness alone. They are a warning. The entity beneath the ocean is awakening, and the corruption spreading through [[Hollowcove]] is only the beginning.
