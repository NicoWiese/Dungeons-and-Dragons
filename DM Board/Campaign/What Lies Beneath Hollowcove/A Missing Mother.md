# Template – Storyline

## Overview
- A little dwarven girl names [[Rosalind Shimmerlock]] is looking for her mom, who went missing 2 weeks ago. She's been staying with [[Thalos Darkbarrel]], [[The Drunken Miner]]'s tavernkeeper, since the disappearance. The villagers are helping her look, and are initially cooperative towards the adventurers. 
- After a while, the villagers become more and more secretive about [[Rosalind Shimmerlock|Rosalind]]'s mom's disappearance, and become less and less willing to help. [[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] eventually asks the adventurers to stop asking questions, saying that [[Rosalind Shimmerlock]] has a new family now. 
- The adventurers keep asking around, and get more and more alienated by the people of [[DM Board/The World/Locations/Hollowcove]]. Following the clues will eventually lead them to [[The Drunken Miner]]'s basement, where they will find [[Jane Shimmerbeard]], locked in a cage between all the ale barrels. The barrels, when inspected, are mostly empty. There are a few full ones that contain black blood, mixed with seaweed and dead fish.

## Clues
- *[[Thalos Darkbarrel]] keeps pointing the adventurers to people who could help in their search, but never helps himself.*
- *He has a very fine kitchen knife called [[Eberhart]] that he uses in his cooking, something he and his grandfather smithed together.*
- *The tavern basement is locked with a small runic seal at the bottom of the door. There is a matching seal on an old wooden door behind the tavern.* 
- *The ale is slowly running out, and the patrons don't seem to care. They don't really eat or drink, they seem to just watch the adventurers.*
- *[[Thalos Darkbarrel]] is always at the bar until exactly 03h00, at which point he walks down into the basement, and isn't seen until  12h00.*
- *[[Thalos Darkbarrel|Thalos]] eventually stops looking at the adventurers, and always has his back to them, or is looking at the floor etc. He also starts smelling like dead fish and rotting flesh.*
- *The other patrons of the tavern start wearing long sleeved clothes and gloves, always making sure to hide their hands. If their gloves get removed, the adventurers will notice similar looking runes carved onto the backs of their hands.*
