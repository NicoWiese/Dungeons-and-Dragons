# Template – Storyline

## Overview

- [[Yannik Hardgust]], a merchant operating out of Brightshore, has lost a valuable shipment that was expected to arrive several days ago.
    
- The shipment vanished somewhere between its point of origin and the city, leaving Yannik desperate to recover both the cargo and the missing crew.
    
- Initial evidence suggests a simple robbery, but further investigation may reveal a more complicated situation.
    
- Tone: Investigation, Exploration, Trade Intrigue
    
- Recommended Party Level: 2–5
    
- Recommended Party Size: 3–6
    

---

## Player Hooks

- [[Yannik Hardgust]] offers payment for recovering the shipment.
    
- The party overhears merchants discussing the missing cargo.
    
- A friend or relative of a missing crew member asks for assistance.
    
- The cargo contains something valuable, dangerous, or politically sensitive.
    

### Immediate Goal

- Locate the missing shipment and determine what happened to it.
    

---

## Key People, Places, and Things

### NPCs

- [[Yannik Hardgust]]
    
    - Role: Merchant and quest giver.
        
    - Motivation: Recover his cargo and avoid financial ruin.
        
    - Secrets: May not be entirely honest about the shipment's contents.
        
    - Personality/quirks: Practical, impatient, always calculating profit margins.
        
- Missing Caravan Captain
    
    - Role: Leader of the missing shipment.
        
    - Motivation: Protect crew and cargo.
        
    - Secrets: Knows information about the disappearance.
        
    - Personality/quirks: Determined and loyal.
        
- Rival Merchant (Optional)
    
    - Role: Potential suspect.
        
    - Motivation: Gain advantage over Yannik.
        
    - Secrets: May be connected to the disappearance.
        
    - Personality/quirks: Charming but evasive.
        

### Locations

- Trade Road
    
    - Description: Main route used by merchants traveling to Brightshore.
        
    - Notable Features:
        
        - Caravan camps.
            
        - Watchtowers.
            
        - Dangerous stretches of wilderness.
            
    - Encounters/events:
        
        - Witness interviews.
            
        - Ambush sites.
            
        - Strange tracks.
            
- Abandoned Campsite
    
    - Description: Last known location of the shipment.
        
    - Notable Features:
        
        - Broken wagons.
            
        - Scattered supplies.
            
        - Signs of struggle.
            
    - Encounters/events:
        
        - Clue gathering.
            
        - Monster tracks.
            
        - Survivor discovery.
            
- Hidden Destination
    
    - Description: Where the shipment ultimately ended up.
        
    - Notable Features:
        
        - Depends on chosen antagonist.
            
    - Encounters/events:
        
        - Final confrontation.
            
        - Cargo recovery.
            

### Important Items / MacGuffins

- Missing Shipment
    
    - Description: Valuable cargo belonging to Yannik.
        
    - Purpose in story: Primary objective.
        
    - Who wants it and why:
        
        - Yannik wants it returned.
            
        - Antagonists want to profit from or use it.
            
- Shipping Manifest
    
    - Description: Official record of cargo contents.
        
    - Purpose in story: Key investigative clue.
        
    - Who wants it and why:
        
        - Merchants, authorities, thieves.

## Central Conflict

### Main Problem

- A valuable shipment has vanished before reaching Brightshore.
    

### Opposition

Choose one or more:

- Bandits.
    
- Pirates.
    
- Smugglers.
    
- Monsters.
    
- Rival merchants.
    
- Corrupted creatures.
    
- Local faction interference.
    

### If Players Do Nothing

- Yannik suffers major financial losses.
    
- Missing crew members are never found.
    
- The cargo falls permanently into enemy hands.
    
- Trade disruptions affect local businesses.
    

---

## Storyline Structure

### Investigation

- Meet Yannik.
    
- Learn shipment details.
    
- Visit last known location.
    

### Tracking

- Follow clues.
    
- Interview witnesses.
    
- Discover who or what intercepted the shipment.
    

### Discovery

- Learn the true fate of the caravan.
    
- Uncover hidden motives behind the disappearance.
    

### Confrontation

- Reach the shipment's location.
    
- Negotiate, infiltrate, or fight.
    
- Secure cargo and survivors.
    

### Resolution

- Return shipment.
    
- Report findings.
    
- Deal with consequences.
    

---

## Background (DM Only)

### True History of Events

- Determine what actually happened to the shipment.
    
- Was it stolen, diverted, destroyed, or hidden?
    

### Hidden Motivations

- Why was the shipment targeted?
    
- Was the cargo itself valuable, or was someone aboard the true target?
    

### Timeline Before Players Arrive

- Shipment departs.
    
- Contact is lost.
    
- Search efforts fail.
    
- Yannik begins hiring outside help.
    
- Adventurers become involved.

## Additional Details

### Random Events

- Broken wagon discovered.
    
- Merchant seeking news.
    
- Strange tracks crossing the road.
    
- Survivor found wandering.
    

### Side Quests

- Recover personal belongings from the shipment.
    
- Escort replacement cargo.
    
- Investigate previous disappearances.
    

### Improvisation Notes

- Allow multiple suspects early on.
    
- Keep the true culprit uncertain until clues accumulate.
    
- Reward creative investigative approaches.
    

### Session-Specific Reminders

- Make the players feel like detectives rather than guards.
    
- Leave several possible explanations early on.
    
- Tie the shipment into wider regional events if desired.
    
- Ensure clues always point toward another lead.
