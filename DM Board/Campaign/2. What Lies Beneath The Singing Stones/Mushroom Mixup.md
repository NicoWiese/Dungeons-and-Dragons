# Template – Storyline

## Overview

A traveling gnome scholar, [[Quagnim Booknose]], has arrived on the [[Singing Stone Isles]] searching for an extraordinarily rare mushroom known as the "Grand Sporecap." After months of research, he believes he has finally located one growing in a nearby cave system.

Unfortunately for everyone involved, Quagnim's "rare mushroom" turns out to be a very confused [[myconid-adult-xmm|Myconid Adult]].

**Tone & Themes:** Adventure, Comedy, Exploration, Misunderstandings

---

## Player Hooks

- [[Quagnim Booknose]] hires the party as guides and bodyguards.
    
- The scholar offers a reward for helping collect the specimen.
    
- Local residents are tired of Quagnim asking everyone about mushrooms and want someone else to deal with him.
    
- The party overhears Quagnim loudly announcing that he has finally discovered a mushroom worth more than a house.
    

### Immediate Goal

- Escort Quagnim to the cave and help him retrieve the "rare mushroom."
    

---

## Key People, Places, and Things

### NPCs

- [[Quagnim Booknose]]
    
    - Role: Quest giver and scholar.
        
    - Motivation: Discover a new species and become famous.
        
    - Secrets: His research is mostly speculation and poorly translated notes.
        
    - Personality/quirks:
        
        - Constantly takes notes.
            
        - Names everything.
            
        - Has absolutely no survival skills.
            
        - Convinced he is an expert.
            
- [[myconid-adult-xmm|Myconid Adult]]
    
    - Role: Accidental target of the expedition.
        
    - Motivation: Live peacefully with its colony.
        
    - Secrets: Has been observing nearby settlements for years.
        
    - Personality/quirks:
        
        - Curious about surface dwellers.
            
        - Speaks slowly.
            
        - Doesn't understand why everyone keeps calling it a mushroom.
            

### Locations

- Mushroom Caverns
    
    - Description: A humid cave network filled with glowing fungi.
        
    - Notable Features:
        
        - Bioluminescent mushrooms.
            
        - Underground streams.
            
        - Dense spore clouds.
            
    - Encounters/events:
        
        - Getting lost.
            
        - Hallucinogenic spores.
            
        - Strange fungal wildlife.
            
- The Myconid Grove
    
    - Description: A hidden chamber where a small myconid colony resides.
        
    - Notable Features:
        
        - Massive mushroom structures.
            
        - Living fungal gardens.
            
        - Spore communication circles.
            
    - Encounters/events:
        
        - First contact with myconids.
            
        - Diplomatic misunderstandings.
            

### Important Items / MacGuffins

- Quagnim's Research Journal
    
    - Description: A notebook full of questionable mushroom sketches.
        
    - Purpose in story: Provides clues and comic relief.
        
    - Who wants it and why:
        
        - Quagnim considers it priceless.
            
- Golden Spore Sample
    
    - Description: A genuinely rare fungal specimen.
        
    - Purpose in story: Alternative reward if the party doesn't capture the myconid.
        
    - Who wants it and why:
        
        - Scholars and alchemists.

## Central Conflict

### Main Problem

- Quagnim believes a sentient creature is an unusually large mushroom and wants to collect it for study.
    

### Opposition

- Misunderstandings.
    
- Protective fungal creatures.
    
- Potentially the myconids themselves if threatened.
    

### If Players Do Nothing

- Quagnim eventually attempts to capture the myconid himself.
    
- Things go badly.
    
- The colony becomes hostile toward outsiders.
    

---

## Storyline Structure

### Hiring

- Meet Quagnim.
    
- Learn about the "rare mushroom."
    
- Travel to the caverns.
    

### Exploration

- Navigate fungal caves.
    
- Avoid hazards.
    
- Follow Quagnim's increasingly questionable directions.
    

### Discovery

- Locate the "specimen."
    
- Realize it is actually a [[myconid-adult-xmm|Myconid Adult]].
    
- Watch Quagnim completely fail to notice.
    

### Resolution

- Convince Quagnim of the truth.
    
- Help establish peaceful contact.
    
- Or attempt to help him capture it (with predictable consequences).
    

---

## Background (DM Only)

### True History of Events

- The myconid colony has lived beneath the islands for generations.
    
- They avoid contact with surface dwellers.
    
- Quagnim's research accidentally led him directly to them.
    

### Hidden Motivations

- Quagnim genuinely wants to advance scientific knowledge.
    
- The myconids simply want to be left alone.
    
- Neither side understands the situation.
    

### Timeline Before Players Arrive

Months Ago:

- Quagnim discovers references to the "Grand Sporecap."
    

Weeks Ago:

- Arrives on the islands.
    

Days Ago:

- Finds evidence leading to the caverns.
    

Today:

- Hires adventurers.

## Concluding the Adventure

### Possible Endings

- Quagnim learns the truth and publishes a groundbreaking paper.
    
- The myconids agree to limited contact with the surface.
    
- Quagnim remains convinced they are "extremely intelligent mushrooms."
    
- Negotiations fail and everyone leaves frustrated.

- New fungal discoveries become available.

## Additional Details

### Random Events

- Talking mushrooms that aren't actually talking.
    
- Spores causing temporary color blindness.
    
- Quagnim getting lost despite holding the map.
    

### Side Quests

- Gather rare ingredients.
    
- Rescue a lost cave explorer.
    
- Investigate strange glowing fungi.
    

### Improvisation Notes

- Lean into comedy.
    
- Let Quagnim be wrong with absolute confidence.
    
- Reward clever non-violent solutions.
    

### Session-Specific Reminders

- The joke is that everyone except Quagnim realizes the truth.
    
- Keep the myconids friendly unless threatened.
    
- Allow the players to decide how long they let Quagnim embarrass himself.
    
- Use the quest as a lighter break from darker campaign themes.
