# Template – Storyline

## Overview

- Several fishermen from [[The Shimmering Reef]] have disappeared while working night shifts offshore.
    
- [[Sildra Kodrith]], a local fisherwoman, believes a ghostly woman appearing on the water is luring sailors to their deaths.
    
- The truth is stranger than simple hauntings: a restless spirit is attempting to warn people away from a dangerous stretch of ocean where something beneath the sea has begun to stir.
    
- Tone: Mystery, Maritime Horror, Investigation
    
- Recommended Party Level: 2–4
    
- Recommended Party Size: 3–5
    

---

## Player Hooks

- [[Sildra Kodrith]] offers payment for investigating the disappearances.
    
- The local fishing industry is suffering, causing concern throughout the settlement.
    
- A friend or relative of a missing fisherman asks the party for help.
    
- The party witnesses the ghostly figure themselves while near the docks.
    

### Immediate Goal

- Discover what happened to the missing fishermen and stop further disappearances.
    

---

## Key People, Places, and Things

### NPCs

- [[Sildra Kodrith]]
    
    - Role: Quest giver and fisherwoman.
        
    - Motivation: Protect her fellow fishermen.
        
    - Secrets: Has seen the ghost herself but fears being called mad.
        
    - Personality/quirks: Practical, blunt, superstitious despite denying it.
        
- [[Loran Tidewalker]]
    
    - Role: Sole survivor of a recent encounter.
        
    - Motivation: Convince people not to sail at night.
        
    - Secrets: Heard whispers beneath the water before falling overboard.
        
    - Personality/quirks: Nervous, sleep-deprived, jumps at sudden noises.
        
- The Ghost Woman
    
    - Role: Apparent antagonist.
        
    - Motivation: Keep sailors away from a dangerous reef.
        
    - Secrets: She is actually trying to save lives.
        
    - Personality/quirks: Silent, mournful, appears only at night.
        

### Locations

- [[The Shimmering Reef]]
    
    - Description: Popular fishing grounds known for colorful fish and coral formations.
        
    - Notable Features:
        
        - Crystal-clear water by day.
            
        - Thick fog at night.
            
        - Strange lights beneath the waves.
            
    - Encounters/events:
        
        - Missing boats.
            
        - Ghost sightings.
            
        - Unnatural whispers.
            
- The Drowned Reef
    
    - Description: Dangerous rocky outcrop beyond the normal fishing area.
        
    - Notable Features:
        
        - Shipwreck debris.
            
        - Strong currents.
            
        - Ancient carved stones submerged beneath the water.
            
    - Encounters/events:
        
        - Ghost appearance.
            
        - Underwater exploration.
            

### Important Items / MacGuffins

- Tarnished Silver Locket
    
    - Description: Waterlogged necklace worn by the ghost.
        
    - Purpose in story: Key to uncovering her identity.
        
    - Who wants it and why:
        
        - The ghost wishes it returned to her remains.
            
- Coral Charm
    
    - Description: Protective trinket recovered from the reef.
        
    - Purpose in story: Evidence of previous disappearances.
        
    - Who wants it and why:
        
        - Families of missing fishermen.

## Central Conflict

### Main Problem

- Fishermen are disappearing after following a mysterious ghostly figure into the sea.
    

### Opposition

- Initially appears to be the ghost.
    
- Actually caused by dangerous creatures and strange influences gathering near the reef.
    

### If Players Do Nothing

- More fishermen disappear.
    
- Fishing operations cease.
    
- Fear spreads through the settlement.
    
- Whatever lurks beneath the reef grows stronger.
    

---

## Storyline Structure

### Investigation

- Speak to fishermen.
    
- Collect eyewitness accounts.
    
- Examine recovered boats.
    

### Discovery

- Learn the ghost appears before every disappearance.
    
- Find evidence connecting her to an old shipwreck.
    

### Night Watch

- Stake out the reef after sunset.
    
- Witness the ghost firsthand.
    
- Follow her into dangerous waters.
    

### Confrontation

- Discover the true source of the disappearances.
    
- Deal with whatever is lurking beneath the reef.
    

### Resolution

- Lay the spirit to rest.
    
- Protect the fishing grounds.
    
- Return to Sildra.
    

---

## Background (DM Only)

### True History of Events

- Decades ago a fishing vessel struck the reef during a storm.
    
- A young woman drowned while attempting to save her crew.
    
- Her spirit became bound to the reef.
    

### Hidden Motivations

- The ghost is not luring sailors to their deaths.
    
- She appears wherever danger is greatest.
    
- The fishermen mistake her warnings for an invitation.
    

### Timeline Before Players Arrive

- Months Ago:
    
    - Strange whispers begin near the reef.
        
- Weeks Ago:
    
    - First fishermen disappear.
        
- Days Ago:
    
    - Multiple sightings of the ghost reported.
        
- Present:
    
    - Fishing boats refuse to sail at night.

### Improvisation Notes

- Present the ghost as threatening at first.
    
- Gradually reveal she is trying to help.
    
- Emphasize isolation and atmosphere over combat.
    

### Session-Specific Reminders

- Make the reef beautiful during the day and unsettling at night.
    
- Allow multiple ways to learn the ghost's true nature.
    
- Tie subtle clues into the larger island corruption storyline.
    
- Keep the final reveal tragic rather than evil.
