# Template – Storyline

## Overview
- Brief summary of the adventure (2–5 sentences)
- Tone and themes (e.g., mystery, horror, political intrigue)
- Recommended party level and size

## Player Hooks
- Why the party gets involved (multiple options if possible)
- Personal stakes or connections to PCs
- Immediate goal presented to players

## Central Conflict
- What is the main problem?
- Who is opposing the players (antagonist/faction)?
- What happens if the players do nothing?

## Storyline Structure

### Act 1 – Introduction
- Starting situation
- Key events or encounters
- Information revealed

### Act 2 – Rising Action
- Complications and twists
- Major encounters (combat, social, exploration)
- Clues and discoveries

### Act 3 – Climax
- Defining confrontation or challenge
- Boss fight or major decision point

### Act 4 – Resolution
- Immediate aftermath
- Consequences of player choices

## Background (DM Only)
- True history of events
- Hidden motivations
- Timeline of events before the players arrive

## Encounters
- Combat encounters (with difficulty notes)
- Social encounters
- Exploration/puzzle elements

## Rewards
- Loot, gold, magic items
- Story rewards (titles, allies, reputation)
- Experience or milestone notes

## Concluding the Adventure
- Possible endings (success, failure, mixed outcomes)
- Long-term consequences
- Hooks for future adventures

## Additional Details
- Random events or encounters
- Side quests
- Improvisation notes
- Session-specific reminders
