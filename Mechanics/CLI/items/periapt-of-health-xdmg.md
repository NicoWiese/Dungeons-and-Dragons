# Periapt of Health
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*  
![](/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/periapt-of-health.webp#right)

- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

While wearing this pendant, you can take a [Magic](/Mechanics/CLI/actions.md#Magic) action to regain `2d4 + 2` [Hit Points](/Mechanics/CLI/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb.md). Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.

In addition, you have [Advantage](/Mechanics/CLI/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md) on saving throws to avoid or end the [Poisoned](/Mechanics/CLI/conditions.md#Poisoned) condition while you wear this pendant.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 284. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
